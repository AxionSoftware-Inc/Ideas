# Governing Equation Discovery

## Core idea
Given experimental/simulation data, discover compact mathematical laws or differential equations while enforcing physical constraints.

## Inputs
- measured time series / spatial fields
- variable meanings and units
- known symmetries/invariants
- candidate operators or allowed structures
- optional prior equations / domain knowledge

## Engine
Combine symbolic regression, sparse identification and operator search with hard constraints from dimensional analysis, symmetry, conservation laws and the Semantic Scientific IR.

## Output
- ranked candidate equations
- fit/error metrics
- dimensional and physical validity report
- uncertainty/confidence
- out-of-sample validation
- simpler equivalent forms
- provenance of every search decision

## Product angle
This is closer to deeptech/IP than a quick SaaS. Current 2026 research is rapidly moving toward physics-informed symbolic regression and automated scientific discovery, so differentiation must come from stronger constraints, verification and operator-level reasoning rather than generic symbolic regression.

## Strategic fit
Very strong fit with an operator-discovery engine: the Atlas can define allowed operators/properties while data provides empirical evidence.

## Monetization
Enterprise R&D licensing, materials/engineering discovery contracts, private on-prem deployment, API for scientific-AI systems.

## MVP
Low-dimensional ODE systems with known benchmark datasets. Require unit correctness and recover known laws before moving to PDEs.
