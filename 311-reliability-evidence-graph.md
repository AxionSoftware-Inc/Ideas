# Reliability Evidence Graph

## Idea
Represent every reliability claim as a dependency on failure modes, tests, field observations, models, assumptions, populations and environments.

## Queries
- Why is MTBF/RUL believed?
- Which field population supports it?
- Which assumption dominates confidence?
- What evidence becomes stale after a design change?

## Strategic value
Common trust layer for PHM, qualification, maintenance and warranty analytics.

## MVP
One component family with test + field failure data.
