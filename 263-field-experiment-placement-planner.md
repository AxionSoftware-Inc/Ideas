# Field Experiment Placement Planner

## Idea
Choose where and when to place field experiments or sensors to maximize information about uncertain crop, soil or management effects.

## Inputs
- current model uncertainty
- spatial soil/terrain variation
- historical yield/weather
- logistics/cost
- candidate treatments

## Outputs
- plot/sensor locations
- treatment allocation
- expected information gain
- redundancy warnings
- cost vs uncertainty reduction

## Buyers
Seed companies, agronomy R&D, large farms and agricultural institutes.

## Moat
Optimal experimental design grounded in spatial agroecosystem semantics.
