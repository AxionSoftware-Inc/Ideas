# Part Qualification Evidence Graph

## Core idea
Represent qualification of a manufactured part as a live evidence graph connecting design requirements, material lots, machine state, process history, in-situ monitoring, post-process measurements, simulations and destructive/non-destructive tests.

## Product
A part is not simply "qualified". The graph explains which evidence supports each requirement and what becomes stale after a design, material, machine or process change.

## Buyers
Aerospace, medical devices, automotive, advanced manufacturing and defense suppliers.

## Economic value
Qualification can require extensive testing and may be repeated after process changes. Semantic impact analysis can support targeted requalification instead of restarting the entire evidence program.

## Moat
Manufacturing digital thread + metrology traceability + model passports + change-impact analysis.
