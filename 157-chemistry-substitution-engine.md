# Chemistry Substitution Engine

## Core idea
Find replacements for solvents, reagents, catalysts or process materials under simultaneous chemistry, safety, cost, availability, sustainability and downstream-purification constraints.

## Product
A substitution is not ranked by structural similarity alone. The engine predicts whether the replacement preserves reaction mechanism and process behavior, then marks evidence strength and required confirmation experiments.

## Buyers
Process chemistry, pharma, specialty chemicals, procurement-constrained R&D and manufacturing.

## Economic value
Supply-chain disruptions and restricted/expensive reagents can force rapid process changes. A decision engine can reduce the experimental search required to qualify alternatives.

## Synergy
Uses Property Intelligence, Literature-to-Executable Knowledge, Optimal Experiment Design and Qualification Planner.
