# Lab-to-Plant Scale-Up Compiler

## Core idea
Translate a successful laboratory reaction into a scale-up model that explicitly tracks which assumptions stop being valid as vessel size, mixing, heat transfer, gas-liquid transfer, residence time and control dynamics change.

## Product
- nondimensionalize the process and identify changing regimes;
- predict heat/mass-transfer bottlenecks;
- flag unsafe or unvalidated operating regions;
- recommend pilot scale and sensor placement;
- generate a scale-up evidence plan rather than a blind scale factor.

## Buyers
Pharma, chemicals, battery/material synthesis, food/process industries and CDMOs.

## Economic value
Scale-up failures are expensive because a chemically correct reaction can become a transport/control problem. The compiler connects chemistry to multiphysics and process qualification.
