# Vibration Measurement Trust Passport

## Goal
Turn a vibration-based diagnosis into a traceable measurement result.

## Passport
- sensor/mounting/orientation
- calibration
- sampling chain
- filtering/windowing
- operating regime
- detectability / probability of detection
- feature provenance
- model validity
- RUL uncertainty

## Value
Condition monitoring becomes audit-ready instead of relying on classifier confidence.

## Buyers
Rotating-equipment operators, OEMs and reliability teams.

## MVP
Bearing/gear vibration workflow.
