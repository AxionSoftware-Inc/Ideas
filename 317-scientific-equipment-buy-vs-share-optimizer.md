# Scientific Equipment Buy-vs-Share Optimizer

## Problem
High-end instruments have acquisition, service, staffing and queueing costs; buying a local instrument is not always cheaper or faster than shared access.

## Product
Compare purchase, lease, external core-facility use and consortium sharing under uncertain demand.

## Outputs
- cost per productive hour
- expected wait time
- utilization
- break-even demand
- staffing/service sensitivity
- research-delay cost

## Buyers
Universities, institutes, hospitals, national labs and R&D companies.

## MVP
Microscopy or mass-spectrometry scenarios.
