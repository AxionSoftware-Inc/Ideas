# Chemical Experiment Preflight Engine

## Core idea
Before a chemistry experiment is executed, compile the planned procedure into a machine-checkable dependency and compatibility model covering equipment limits, sequencing, temperature/pressure envelopes, materials compatibility, measurement readiness and missing procedural information.

## Product
The system flags inconsistent quantities, incompatible equipment capabilities, unbound variables, impossible timing, missing analytical checkpoints and departures from validated operating envelopes. It can simulate process-state trajectories where models exist.

## Buyers
Industrial R&D, automated chemistry labs, process development and teaching/research labs.

## Strategic value
This is the chemistry specialization of the Experiment Preflight Sandbox. The goal is not generic hazard advice; it is rigorous workflow validation, reproducibility and machine-executable readiness before scarce lab time is consumed.
