# Material Process Recipe Compiler

## Goal
Given desired material properties, generate candidate processing routes rather than only candidate compositions.

Input:
- target strength/toughness/conductivity
- allowed chemistry
- available manufacturing equipment
- cost and cycle-time constraints

Output:
- candidate heat treatments / sintering / deposition / additive-manufacturing schedules
- expected microstructure and properties
- uncertainty
- validation experiments
- process windows rather than single magic recipes

## Difference from inverse design
The target is a manufacturable process recipe tied to equipment and quality constraints, not only an ideal material structure.

## Business
Metals, ceramics, batteries, semiconductors, composites and additive manufacturing.

## Strategic value
Closes the loop from material science to factory execution.
