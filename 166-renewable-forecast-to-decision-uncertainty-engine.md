# Renewable Forecast-to-Decision Uncertainty Engine

## Core idea
Carry forecast uncertainty from weather/renewable generation models all the way into dispatch, reserve, storage and market decisions instead of presenting point forecasts alone.

## Product
Represent calibrated probability distributions and scenario structure, detect regime shifts, quantify the cost of forecast error and choose decisions that are robust to uncertainty.

## Buyers
Renewable operators, utilities, aggregators, storage operators and energy traders.

## Economic value
The value of a forecast is decision-dependent. The system optimizes the downstream action and can measure whether a more expensive forecast source is worth buying.

## Moat
Unify model validity, uncertainty, decision optimization and data valuation rather than competing on weather prediction alone.
