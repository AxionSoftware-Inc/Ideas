# CFD Model Trust Passport

## Problem
A CFD result can look precise while depending strongly on mesh, turbulence closure, boundary conditions, numerics and validation regime.

## Product
Generate a machine-readable trust passport for a CFD case: governing equations, closures, mesh convergence evidence, BC assumptions, solver settings, validation comparisons, uncertainty and valid operating envelope.

## Buyers
Aerospace, automotive, energy, turbomachinery and engineering consultancies.

## Material value
Faster design reviews and fewer decisions based on visually convincing but weak simulations.

## Moat
Unified simulation evidence rather than solver-specific reports.

## MVP
Wrap OpenFOAM-like cases and score trust from mesh/solver/reference evidence.
