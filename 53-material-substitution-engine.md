# Material Substitution Engine

## Goal
Find qualified replacements when a material becomes too expensive, unavailable, regulated, geographically risky or technically undesirable.

## Inputs
- current material and application
- load/temperature/environment
- manufacturing process
- certification requirements
- supply-chain and cost constraints

## Output
A ranked substitution plan with:
- closest functional alternatives
- property gaps
- design changes required
- validation tests required
- uncertainty and risk
- estimated qualification burden

## Strategic value
Turns the materials knowledge layer into a procurement and engineering decision system rather than a research-only database.

## Business model
Enterprise subscription or per-project analysis for manufacturers, automotive, aerospace, energy and industrial supply chains.
