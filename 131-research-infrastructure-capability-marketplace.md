# Research Infrastructure Capability Marketplace

## Thesis
Make laboratories, pilot lines, clean rooms, instruments, compute facilities and expert services searchable by machine-readable capability rather than institution name.

## Query example
"Need Raman spectroscopy, 532 nm, temperature-controlled stage, samples under inert atmosphere, turnaround < 10 days."

## System returns
compatible facilities, capability evidence, access rules, lead time, price/request process, data formats and integration requirements.

## Buyers
Research infrastructures, universities, industrial R&D, startups.

## Business
Marketplace/lead-generation fee, institutional SaaS, enterprise procurement integration.
