# Battery Second-Life Qualification Engine

## Core idea
Determine whether used EV/industrial batteries are suitable for specific second-life applications using physics-aware health inference, uncertainty, pack heterogeneity and target-duty requirements.

## Product
Given field history and diagnostic tests, estimate usable energy/power, degradation mechanisms, safety margin, expected remaining life and application-specific confidence. Recommend reuse, module sorting, lower-demand application or recycling.

## Buyers
Battery recyclers, second-life integrators, fleets, storage developers and OEMs.

## Economic value
Second-life value depends on avoiding both false rejection of useful batteries and expensive deployment of unreliable ones. The engine converts incomplete health data into application-specific qualification evidence.

## Synergy
Battery Twin + Measurement Trust + Model Passport + Material/Asset Qualification Planner.
