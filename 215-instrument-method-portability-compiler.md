# Instrument Method Portability Compiler

## Problem
A validated measurement method is often tied to one vendor/model. Moving to another instrument requires manual reinterpretation of settings, ranges, filters and calibration assumptions.

## Product
Translate an abstract measurement method onto a target instrument capability profile while identifying unsupported semantics, changed uncertainty and required revalidation.

## Buyers
Labs with mixed fleets, instrument vendors, contract labs and regulated test organizations.

## Material value
Lower equipment-switching cost and faster method transfer.

## Moat
A semantic method representation independent of vendor command languages.

## MVP
Translate one measurement workflow between two simulated instrument models.
