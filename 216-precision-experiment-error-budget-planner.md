# Precision Experiment Error Budget Planner

## Problem
In high-precision experiments, teams may optimize components that contribute little to total uncertainty while ignoring dominant systematic effects.

## Product
Build a complete uncertainty/error budget before hardware is purchased or experiments are run, rank dominant contributors and recommend where additional precision actually buys information.

## Buyers
Precision physics labs, metrology institutes, sensor companies and advanced R&D.

## Material value
Spend instrumentation budget where it matters and prevent impossible accuracy targets.

## Moat
Semantic propagation across instrument, environment, calibration and model uncertainty.

## MVP
Plan a synthetic precision measurement and optimize cost versus target uncertainty.
