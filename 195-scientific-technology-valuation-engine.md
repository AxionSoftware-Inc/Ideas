# Scientific Technology Valuation Engine

## Core idea
Estimate the value of a deep-tech/scientific asset from evidence rather than generic market multiples: technical uniqueness, reproducibility, readiness, replacement cost, performance delta, IP position, integration burden, market alternatives and de-risking milestones.

## Product
Produce scenario-based valuation ranges for license, assignment, spinout or strategic partnership and show which technical milestone has the highest expected value-of-information.

## Buyers
TTOs, deep-tech founders, investors, corporate innovation and R&D portfolio managers.

## Important boundary
This is a decision-support and evidence system, not an authoritative legal or financial valuation.

## Moat
Scientific evidence graph + IP dependency + technology readiness + market/alternative model.
