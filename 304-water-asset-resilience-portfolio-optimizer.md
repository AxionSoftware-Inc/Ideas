# Water Asset Resilience Portfolio Optimizer

## Idea
Allocate limited utility capital across pipes, pumps, treatment units, storage, sensors and emergency measures using physics-informed risk.

## Inputs
Failure probabilities, hydraulic consequence, service criticality, climate scenarios, maintenance cost and uncertainty.

## Outputs
- budget-constrained intervention portfolio
- avoided expected outage/loss
- resilience under extreme scenarios
- information-gathering investments
- projects dominated by better alternatives

## MVP
Distribution network renewal planning.
