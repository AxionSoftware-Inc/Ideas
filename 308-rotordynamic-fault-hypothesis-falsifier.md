# Rotordynamic Fault Hypothesis Falsifier

## Goal
Given vibration changes, generate and pressure-test physical hypotheses such as imbalance, misalignment, looseness, rub, bearing damage or resonance shift.

## Method
Each hypothesis predicts signatures across speed, phase, orders, orbit shape and sensor positions. Compare those predictions with evidence and reject inconsistent causes.

## Output
- surviving hypotheses
- falsified causes and reasons
- uncertainty
- discriminating next test

## MVP
Rotor-bearing test rig or historical industrial datasets.
