# Quantitative Imaging Semantic IR

Represent an image as a measurement object, not merely pixels.

Core fields include specimen identity, preparation, optics, illumination, detector, objective, point-spread function, exposure, gain, calibration, spatial scale, spectral channels, processing history, uncertainty and biological/physical context.

This IR can power reproducibility, cross-instrument comparison, image-derived measurements and auditability.

**MVP:** fluorescence microscopy with spatial/intensity calibration and processing provenance.
