# CFD-to-Test Correlation Engine

## Problem
Simulation and wind-tunnel/rig measurements often disagree because geometry, sensor filtering, boundary conditions and test corrections are not semantically aligned.

## Product
Align simulation observables with test instrumentation and automatically decompose disagreement into likely model, setup, geometry and measurement causes.

## Buyers
Aerospace, automotive, turbomachinery and test organizations.

## Material value
Shorten correlation campaigns and avoid tuning models to mismatched measurements.

## Moat
Shared semantic representation of simulated and measured observables with uncertainty.

## MVP
Correlate one canonical flow case against synthetic noisy test data.
