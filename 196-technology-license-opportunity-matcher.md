# Technology License Opportunity Matcher

## Core idea
Match scientific IP to companies that have the technical capability, product gap, market incentive and integration context to license it—not merely companies sharing keywords.

## Product
Represent both technology and potential licensee by capabilities, architectures, constraints, existing products, manufacturing assets and strategic needs. Rank matches and generate the technical reason each company could absorb the invention.

## Buyers
TTOs, research institutes, deep-tech inventors and corporate scouting teams.

## Economic value
Technology transfer often fails because the right buyer never sees or understands the asset. Capability-based matching can improve outbound licensing efficiency.

## Moat
Scientific semantic graph + company/product capability graph + IP/technology readiness evidence.
