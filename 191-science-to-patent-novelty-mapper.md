# Science-to-Patent Novelty Mapper

## Core idea
Map a technical result against papers, patents, product architectures and known technical approaches to identify potentially novel technical cores and crowded areas before expensive commercialization work.

## Product
Represent an invention as a graph of mechanisms, components, constraints and claims-to-evidence candidates. Search for semantically similar prior technical structures even when terminology differs, then produce a landscape for expert review.

## Buyers
Deep-tech startups, university TTOs, R&D organizations, patent professionals and corporate innovation teams.

## Value
The tool does not decide patentability. It reduces search and framing cost, highlights likely novelty boundaries and helps teams decide which technical branch deserves deeper professional analysis.

## Moat
Scientific semantic search + theorem/model equivalence + IP dependency graph.
