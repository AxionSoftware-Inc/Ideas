# Remote-Sensing-to-Physics Assimilation Compiler

## Core idea
Compile satellite/airborne/ground observations into assimilation-ready physical variables with sensor physics, spatial support, timing, units, quality flags and uncertainty preserved.

## Product
Automatically connect observations to compatible state variables and forward operators, detect representativeness mismatches and generate the transformation/observation model required by an inverse problem or digital twin.

## Buyers
Earth observation, agriculture, hydrology, environmental monitoring, climate services and geoscience R&D.

## Strategic value
The expensive manual bridge is often not obtaining imagery but turning heterogeneous measurements into scientifically valid model constraints. This becomes a reusable semantic adapter between observation infrastructure and physics models.
