# Remaining Useful Life Calibration Passport

## Problem
An RUL model can rank assets well yet be badly calibrated in actual remaining time, especially under domain shift.

## Product
Evaluate and continuously document RUL calibration by regime, failure mechanism and population.

## Passport
- prediction intervals vs observed failures
- coverage calibration
- OOD status
- censoring assumptions
- operating-environment validity
- retraining/change history

## Output
Not merely 'RUL = 120 h', but '120 h with calibrated evidence in this regime'.

## MVP
Bearing or battery run-to-failure benchmark.
