# Reaction Route Feasibility & Scale-Up Gate

## Core idea
Score a proposed synthesis route not only for chemical plausibility, but for reproducibility, scale-up risk, reagent availability, isolation complexity, heat/mass-transfer sensitivity, analytical burden and evidence quality.

## Product
For every route, return a structured `GO / PILOT / HIGH-RISK / NO-GO` decision with the assumptions that drive it and the minimum experiments needed to reduce uncertainty.

## Buyers
Process chemistry, pharma, fine chemicals, materials companies, CRO/CDMO organizations.

## Economic value
A route that works once in a paper may be a poor manufacturing route. Filtering weak routes before pilot work can save weeks of chemist and reactor time.

## Moat
Combine literature evidence, semantic reaction records, model validity, uncertainty and facility capabilities rather than using retrosynthesis score alone.
