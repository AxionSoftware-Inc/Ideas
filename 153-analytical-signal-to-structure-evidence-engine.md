# Analytical Signal-to-Structure Evidence Engine

## Core idea
Fuse NMR, MS, IR/Raman, UV-Vis, chromatography and other analytical evidence into a structured confidence graph for molecular identity, purity and competing hypotheses.

## Product
Instead of returning one guessed structure, maintain candidate structures with evidence for/against each assignment, unresolved peaks, impurity hypotheses and confidence by substructure.

## Buyers
Synthetic chemistry labs, pharma, materials labs, analytical service companies and autonomous labs.

## Strategic value
Connect raw analytical measurements to Scientific Provenance Graph and Reaction Semantic IR. This creates a machine-readable evidence layer between "reaction completed" and "product identity verified".

## Differentiation
The moat is cross-instrument evidence accounting and uncertainty, not a single spectrum predictor.
