# Strain-to-Plant Technoeconomic Viability Gate

## Goal
Stop optimizing biological metrics that do not survive process and economic reality.

## Pipeline
`strain metrics -> scale-up physics -> downstream recovery -> utilities -> cycle time -> yield uncertainty -> cost/revenue -> viability`

## Output
- minimum viable titer/rate/yield
- dominant cost sensitivities
- scale-up risk premium
- break-even ranges
- experiments with highest economic value
- GO / RESEARCH / NO-GO

## Strategic value
Connects synthetic biology directly to commercial decisions instead of treating DBTL and technoeconomics as separate worlds.

## MVP
Commodity biochemical or fermentation product with simple downstream processing.
