# Storage Revenue / Risk Planner

## Core idea
Evaluate an energy-storage project as a coupled physical-financial system before investment or operating-strategy changes.

## Product
Combine degradation models, tariff/market opportunities, cycling constraints, uncertainty, warranty terms, site load and grid-service eligibility. Produce revenue distributions, lifetime throughput, replacement timing and dominant risks.

## Buyers
BESS developers, investors, commercial facilities, microgrids and fleet operators.

## Economic value
Conventional spreadsheets often separate battery physics from financial assumptions. This product makes every financial scenario traceable to physical behavior and uncertainty.

## Strategic value
A strong wedge into finance/asset management for the larger scientific twin platform because the output is directly expressed as expected cashflow and risk.
