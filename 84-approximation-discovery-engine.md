# Approximation Discovery Engine

## Problem
Useful approximations are often discovered by expert intuition: identify a regime, discard small terms, choose a representation, derive an asymptotic form, then estimate error.

## Product
Automatically search for simpler models or formulas that approximate a target system in a declared regime.

## Search dimensions
- nondimensionalization
- dominant balance
- asymptotic limits
- perturbative truncation
- low-rank/operator approximations
- rational/series approximants
- reduced-order models
- matched local approximations

## Output
Candidate approximation + validity region + symbolic/numeric error bound or empirical error map + derivation certificate.

## Value
Turns expensive or opaque models into fast, interpretable engineering formulas.

## Strategic role
Compiler optimization for mathematics: preserve required behavior while minimizing expression/model complexity and compute cost.
