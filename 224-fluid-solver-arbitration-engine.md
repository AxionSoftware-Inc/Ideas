# Fluid Solver Arbitration Engine

## Problem
Teams choose among reduced-order models, 1D networks, RANS, LES, particle methods and specialized solvers with limited quantitative guidance on cost versus decision accuracy.

## Product
Given target observables, regime, geometry, uncertainty and compute budget, rank solver/model families and escalate fidelity only where evidence demands it.

## Buyers
Engineering simulation teams, cloud/HPC platforms and consultancies.

## Material value
Avoid both under-modeling and expensive unnecessary high-fidelity runs.

## Moat
Benchmark registry + validity knowledge + decision-specific accuracy requirements.

## MVP
Arbitrate between a simple analytical model, RANS-like solver and higher-fidelity reference for canonical flows.
