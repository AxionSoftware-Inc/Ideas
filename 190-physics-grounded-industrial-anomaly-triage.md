# Physics-Grounded Industrial Anomaly Triage

## Core idea
When industrial AI flags an anomaly, separate three possibilities: the physical process changed, the measurement chain degraded, or the data/model pipeline is wrong.

## Product
Fuse sensor trust passports, process models, redundant measurements, conservation/constraint checks and historical evidence. Return ranked causes and recommended diagnostic actions instead of an opaque anomaly score.

## Buyers
Continuous manufacturing, battery/coating lines, energy/process plants and predictive-maintenance teams.

## Economic value
False alarms create downtime while missed process faults create scrap or failures. Evidence-aware triage reduces both.

## Why timely
Trustworthy industrial AI requires traceability, standardized uncertainty, explainability and reproducibility across measurement and decision pipelines.

## Moat
Measurement assurance + physics constraints + digital twin + causal evidence graph.
