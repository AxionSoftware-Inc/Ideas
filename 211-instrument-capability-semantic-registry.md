# Instrument Capability Semantic Registry

## Problem
Scientific instruments are described by manuals and vendor-specific APIs, but software agents and workflow systems lack a normalized understanding of what each instrument can measure, under what conditions and with what uncertainty.

## Product
A typed registry of instrument capabilities, ranges, calibration status, sample constraints, measurement models, uncertainty and control interfaces.

## Buyers
Labs, core facilities, instrument vendors and autonomous-lab platforms.

## Strategic value
Becomes a discovery/runtime layer for scientific agents and experiment planners.

## Moat
Machine-readable capability semantics plus real calibration/performance evidence.

## MVP
Normalize 5–10 common lab instruments into one capability schema.
