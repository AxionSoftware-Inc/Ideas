# Water Quality Causal Provenance Graph

## Idea
Track how a water-quality claim depends on source conditions, sampling, calibration, transport, treatment, models and laboratory assays.

## Queries
- Why is contaminant concentration believed to be X?
- Which measurement supports this conclusion?
- What changed since the previous report?
- Which downstream decisions become stale if a calibration is invalidated?

## Value
Useful for utilities, environmental compliance, incident investigation and contested evidence.

## MVP
One contaminant across source-to-treatment-to-distribution chain.
