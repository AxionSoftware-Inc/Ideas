# Manufacturing Yield / Tolerance Co-Optimizer

## Core idea
Optimize product tolerances and process capability together instead of treating design tolerances as fixed inputs to manufacturing.

## Product
Use sensitivity models, process distributions, measurement uncertainty, failure cost and functional requirements to identify where tolerances can be relaxed, where they must tighten and where process investment yields the greatest business value.

## Buyers
Product engineering, manufacturing engineering, suppliers and cost-reduction teams.

## Economic value
Over-tight tolerances waste money; under-tight tolerances create failure. The engine quantifies this trade-off at system level and can propose design/process changes with predicted yield and cost impact.

## Synergy
Tolerance & Uncertainty Budget Optimizer + Requirements-to-Physics + process capability evidence.
