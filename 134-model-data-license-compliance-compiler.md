# Model & Data License Compliance Compiler

## Thesis
Compile scientific asset licenses and usage restrictions into machine-checkable deployment/distribution policies.

## Checks
- commercial use
- derivative/redistribution rights
- attribution
- data residency/access terms
- incompatible dependency chains
- model/data combination restrictions

## Output
An auditable decision explaining whether a model/workflow may be shipped, sold, published or used internally.

## Buyers
Deeptech companies, publishers, scientific platforms, legal/technology-transfer teams.

## Guardrail
Assist compliance workflows; do not present automated interpretation as legal advice.
