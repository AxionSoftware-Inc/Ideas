# Modal Identity & Change Tracker

## Problem
Modes can cross, split or change shape as structures or machines vary, making naive frequency tracking misleading.

## Product
Maintain semantic identity of modes across operating conditions, model revisions and measurements.

## Signals
Frequency, damping, mode shape, symmetry, localization, participation and physical component association.

## Outputs
- persistent mode IDs
- true structural change vs tracking artifact
- uncertainty
- change provenance

## Uses
Structures, turbomachinery, vehicles, acoustics and test correlation.

## MVP
Experimental/FE modal data matching.
