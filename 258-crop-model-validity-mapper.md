# Crop Model Validity Mapper

## Idea
Map where a crop or agroecosystem model is trustworthy across cultivar, soil, climate, management regime and growth stage.

## Outputs
- validated region
- extrapolation warnings
- uncertainty surfaces
- missing field-validation zones
- most valuable next calibration data

## Buyers
Ag-tech companies, crop-model developers, insurers and research programs.

## Moat
Domain-of-validity reasoning across biological, environmental and management variables.
