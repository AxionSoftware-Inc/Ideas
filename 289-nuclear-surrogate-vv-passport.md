# Nuclear Surrogate V&V Passport

## Problem
Fast ML/reduced-order surrogates can be accurate inside a training region yet unsafe to trust outside it.

## Product
A regulator-oriented passport for each surrogate model.

## Evidence
- reference-code comparisons
- physics-consistency checks
- uncertainty calibration
- OOD detection
- interpolation/extrapolation domains
- training reproducibility
- numerical tolerances
- intended safety role
- review provenance

## Output
Explicit admissible uses and prohibited uses rather than one accuracy score.

## Buyers
Reactor vendors, national labs, utilities and regulators.

## MVP
Reactor-physics or thermal-hydraulics surrogate benchmark.
