# R&D Milestone Evidence Compiler

## Core idea
Turn grants, internal R&D programs and investor milestones into machine-checkable scientific evidence requirements instead of narrative progress reports.

## Product
Each milestone specifies claims, metrics, test conditions, baselines, reproducibility requirements and evidence sources. The system continuously tracks whether the milestone is genuinely satisfied, what remains missing and whether later changes invalidate earlier evidence.

## Buyers
Corporate R&D, grant programs, deep-tech investors, government innovation agencies and multi-partner research consortia.

## Economic value
Funding decisions become tied to reproducible technical progress rather than document quality alone. It also reduces reporting overhead by generating progress views directly from the provenance/evidence graph.

## Strategic value
This is a commercialization/funding interface for the Scientific OS and can make the platform valuable before the technology reaches product deployment.
