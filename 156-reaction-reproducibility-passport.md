# Reaction Reproducibility Passport

## Core idea
Attach a reproducibility score and evidence packet to each reaction procedure.

## Passport fields
Completeness of conditions, reagent identity/purity, equipment assumptions, addition order, temperature/mixing history, workup, analytical verification, replication count, operator/lab transfer evidence and known failure modes.

## Product
Before another lab repeats a published or internal method, the system identifies which hidden variables are likely to prevent reproduction and asks for the minimum missing information.

## Buyers
Publishers, pharma/process R&D, CROs, shared laboratories and research institutions.

## Strategic value
Negative and transfer results feed back into the Reaction Semantic IR and evidence graph, gradually creating a high-value dataset of what actually makes chemistry reproducible.
