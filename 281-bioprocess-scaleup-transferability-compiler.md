# Bioprocess Scale-Up Transferability Compiler

## Problem
Lab-optimized strains and processes can underperform in industrial bioreactors because mixing, oxygen, heat, shear and substrate gradients change the biological operating environment.

## Product
Compile lab evidence, strain physiology, reactor geometry and process conditions into a scale-up transferability report.

`lab process -> reactor gradients -> cellular stress -> yield/productivity risk -> scale-up evidence plan`

## Outputs
- gradient exposure map
- scale-dependent failure mechanisms
- expected productivity range
- missing evidence
- recommended scale-down experiments
- GO / PILOT / REWORK decision

## Buyers
Biomanufacturing, fermentation, biopharma, industrial biotechnology and CDMOs.

## Moat
A growing map from reactor physics to biological response plus validated transfer cases.

## MVP
One microbial fermentation class with oxygen-transfer, substrate-gradient and heat-removal reasoning.
