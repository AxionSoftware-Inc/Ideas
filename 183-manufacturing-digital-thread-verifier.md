# Manufacturing Digital Thread Verifier

## Core idea
Continuously verify that design intent survives the full path from CAD/PMI through process planning, machine instructions, inspection, product passport and field service.

## Product
Detect semantic loss, unit/convention mismatches, stale revisions, broken identifiers, untraceable manual edits and requirements that never receive manufacturing or inspection evidence.

## Buyers
Complex manufacturing, aerospace supply chains, automotive, industrial equipment and PLM/QMS vendors.

## Value
A digital thread can exist technically while silently losing meaning between systems. The verifier treats lifecycle information transfer like a compiler/linker problem and produces conformance diagnostics.

## Strategic value
A cross-domain bridge between BIM/CAD semantics, data contracts, product passports, metrology and Scientific Change-Impact Analyzer.
