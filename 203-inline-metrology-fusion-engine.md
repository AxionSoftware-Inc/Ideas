# Inline Metrology Fusion Engine

## Problem
A fab may measure the same latent process state through CD-SEM, scatterometry, ellipsometry, electrical tests and equipment sensors, each with different uncertainty and coverage.

## Product
Fuse heterogeneous inline measurements into one uncertainty-aware estimate of critical process state and detect disagreement between instruments before it becomes yield loss.

## Buyers
Fabs, metrology vendors and process-control teams.

## Material value
Fewer redundant measurements, earlier drift detection and better virtual metrology.

## Moat
Cross-instrument calibration, uncertainty propagation and process-context-aware fusion rather than generic sensor fusion.

## MVP
Fuse two or three measurement modalities for film thickness/CD estimation with confidence bounds.
