# Electrochemical Degradation-Mechanism Arbitrator

Maintain competing explanations for electrochemical degradation instead of forcing one fitted equivalent circuit.

Examples:
SEI growth, lithium plating, catalyst poisoning, corrosion, membrane dehydration, gas crossover, contact loss or transport limitation.

Fuse EIS, cycling history, temperature, pressure, gas analysis, microscopy and mechanistic models to rank hypotheses and recommend discriminating tests.
