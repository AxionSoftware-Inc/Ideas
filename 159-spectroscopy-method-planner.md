# Spectroscopy Method Planner

## Core idea
Given a scientific identification/quantification goal and candidate hypotheses, choose the analytical measurements that are expected to provide the most discriminating information per unit cost/time/sample.

## Product
Rather than automatically collecting every spectrum, estimate which NMR experiment, MS mode, IR/Raman region, chromatography method or other measurement most reduces uncertainty among competing structures/process states.

## Buyers
Analytical labs, chemistry/materials R&D, autonomous laboratories and quality control.

## Strategic value
This is Optimal Experiment Design applied to analytical chemistry. It closes a loop: candidate hypotheses -> best measurement -> evidence update -> next measurement.

## Moat
Evidence graph plus instrument capability/cost registry and semantic models of what each measurement can resolve.
