# Battery Pack Heterogeneity Diagnoser

## Core idea
Infer which cells/modules are diverging inside a pack, why they are diverging and how much pack-level capability is being lost because the weakest components constrain the whole system.

## Product
Fuse voltage, current, temperature, balancing activity, impedance/diagnostic data and pack topology into a hidden-state model. Rank anomalous modules and distinguish measurement error, capacity fade, resistance growth, thermal imbalance and connection problems.

## Buyers
EV fleets, stationary storage, battery service/second-life companies and OEMs.

## Value
Prioritize inspection/replacement, improve balancing and avoid discarding an entire pack when a small subset creates the limitation.

## Moat
Pack topology + mechanism-aware twin + uncertainty + fleet-level comparative evidence.
