# Telescope / Instrument Calibration Provenance Engine

## Idea
Track every calibration product and correction applied to astronomical or space-science observations as a dependency graph.

## Core value
A calibration update should automatically identify which spectra, images, catalogs and published measurements are stale.

## Tracks
- detector state
- calibration exposures
- reference standards
- pipeline versions
- correction models
- uncertainty propagation
- derived science products

## Buyers
Observatories, mission archives and survey collaborations.

## Moat
Calibration-to-claim lineage with automatic impact analysis.
