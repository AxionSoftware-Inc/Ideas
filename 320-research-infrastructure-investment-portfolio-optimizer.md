# Research Infrastructure Investment Portfolio Optimizer

## Goal
Allocate a capital budget across scientific equipment, compute, labs and shared facilities based on research value rather than wish lists.

## Inputs
Demand forecasts, capability gaps, queueing, costs, maintenance, staffing, project dependencies and strategic priorities.

## Outputs
- ranked investments
- projects enabled by each asset
- utilization and congestion forecasts
- option value of sharing or delaying purchase
- portfolio under budget

## Buyers
Universities, national labs, governments and corporate R&D.

## MVP
One institute with 10–30 candidate equipment investments.
