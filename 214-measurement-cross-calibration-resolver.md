# Measurement Cross-Calibration Resolver

## Problem
Two instruments can disagree without an obvious reference telling the lab which one is wrong, especially when environmental or sample effects are involved.

## Product
Use reference standards, redundant instruments, historical behavior and measurement models to infer likely bias sources and propose the cheapest discriminating calibration/check.

## Buyers
Metrology labs, test labs, manufacturing and scientific facilities.

## Material value
Reduce downtime and unnecessary service while preventing bad data propagation.

## Moat
Evidence-based bias attribution across heterogeneous instruments.

## MVP
Resolve injected bias among three simulated sensors using redundant measurements and uncertainty models.
