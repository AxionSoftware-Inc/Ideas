# Farm Semantic Digital Twin

## Idea
Represent crop, soil, irrigation, machinery, weather, inputs, operations and economics in one typed farm-state model.

## Difference from dashboards
The twin understands relationships: which irrigation action affects which soil zone, crop stage, nutrient state, yield model and cost.

## Core objects
- fields/zones
- crop stages
- soil layers
- sensors
- irrigation/fertilization events
- machinery
- weather/forecast
- yield/economic objectives

## Buyers
Large farms, ag-tech platforms, irrigation providers and agricultural research programs.

## Moat
Cross-asset farm semantics and lifecycle state, not isolated sensor dashboards.
