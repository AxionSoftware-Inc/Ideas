# Compliance Change-Impact Engine

## Idea
When a product design, process, software version or standard changes, determine exactly which compliance obligations and evidence need re-evaluation.

## Outputs
- affected clauses
- stale tests and simulations
- required requalification scope
- unaffected evidence that can be reused
- estimated cost/time impact

## Buyers
Regulated engineering companies and certification teams.

## Moat
Semantic diff across product, model, evidence and standards graphs.
