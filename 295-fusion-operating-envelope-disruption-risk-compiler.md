# Fusion Operating Envelope & Disruption-Risk Compiler

## Goal
Map where candidate plasma operating points are both performant and sufficiently supported by control/physics evidence.

## Output
- feasible operating region
- proximity to instability/disruption boundaries
- uncertainty margins
- diagnostic coverage
- control authority
- experiment priorities that shrink unsafe uncertainty

## Principle
Do not produce a false deterministic safe/unsafe label when evidence is weak; expose confidence and admissibility.

## MVP
Reduced tokamak scenario model with a small set of operational constraints.
