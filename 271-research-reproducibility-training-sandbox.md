# Research Reproducibility Training Sandbox

## Idea
Train researchers by giving them intentionally incomplete or fragile computational experiments and asking them to make the result reproducible.

## Scenarios
- missing dependency/version
- hidden random seed
- undocumented preprocessing
- unavailable data artifact
- unit mismatch
- non-portable environment
- stale figure

## Output
A reproducibility competency score plus exact failure explanations.

## Buyers
Universities, research institutes and corporate R&D onboarding.

## Moat
Uses the same provenance/lockfile/replay infrastructure as production scientific workflows.
