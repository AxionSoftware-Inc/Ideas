# Drift-Aware Instrument Health Twin

## Problem
Calibration certificates are snapshots. Instruments drift between calibrations, but labs often lack a quantitative live estimate of measurement trust.

## Product
Maintain a state estimate for each instrument using calibration history, check standards, environment, usage, repairs and cross-comparisons. Predict drift risk and confidence degradation.

## Buyers
Calibration labs, regulated labs, manufacturing metrology and research facilities.

## Material value
Avoid both premature recalibration and silent out-of-tolerance operation.

## Moat
Instrument-specific drift models linked to traceability and decision risk.

## MVP
Demonstrate on a simulated balance, thermometer or DMM fleet.
