# Nuclear Transient Scenario Stress Tester

## Goal
Systematically search uncertain operating/transient conditions for cases where models, controls or safety margins degrade.

## Inputs
Coupled physics model, uncertainty distributions, operational constraints and candidate failure scenarios.

## Outputs
- worst credible cases
- margin erosion map
- model disagreement zones
- OOD warnings for surrogates
- high-value validation scenarios

## Strategic value
Turns scenario analysis from manually selected cases into evidence-directed falsification.

## MVP
Simplified reactor thermal-hydraulic transient suite.
