# Mechanism-Aware Battery Health Twin

## Core idea
A battery twin that represents degradation as coupled electrochemical, thermal, mechanical and interfacial mechanisms rather than one scalar State-of-Health number.

## Product
Continuously infer hidden degradation states from sparse operational data, carry uncertainty, update parameters as the cell ages and expose which mechanism is limiting power, fast charging, safety or remaining life.

## Buyers
EV/BMS companies, stationary storage operators, battery manufacturers, fleet operators and testing labs.

## Value
The decision question becomes not only "SOH = 82%" but "what is degrading, how certain are we, which action causes the least further damage, and how much usable capability remains?"

## Moat
Semantic coupling of physics models, uncertainty, measurements, field history and decision-facing control.
