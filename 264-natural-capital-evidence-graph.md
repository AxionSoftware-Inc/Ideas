# Natural Capital Evidence Graph

## Idea
Connect farm and landscape management actions to measurable soil, biodiversity, water and ecosystem-service outcomes with explicit evidence and uncertainty.

## Why it matters
Natural-capital claims are often disconnected from operational farm data and causal evidence.

## Graph links
- management action
- field/landscape state
- measurements
- ecological models
- uncertainty
- economic/credit claims
- monitoring history

## Buyers
Large farms, sustainability programs, insurers, lenders and environmental markets.

## Moat
Traceable action-to-ecological-outcome evidence rather than sustainability dashboards.
