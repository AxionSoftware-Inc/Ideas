# Supplier Process Capability Passport

## Core idea
A machine-readable evidence passport for a supplier/process that describes what it can reliably manufacture—not just certifications or nominal machine specs.

## Passport
Validated materials/geometries, process windows, tolerance capability, measurement system, uncertainty, defect history, qualification evidence, equipment/software versions and change history.

## Buyers
Aerospace/automotive OEMs, procurement teams, contract manufacturing networks and regulated supply chains.

## Economic value
Supplier selection and requalification can become evidence-based. Requirements can be matched automatically against demonstrated process capability before RFQ or production transfer.

## Moat
Links product requirements directly to verified manufacturing evidence and can feed a future marketplace for qualified scientific/manufacturing capabilities.
