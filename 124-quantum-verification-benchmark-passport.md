# Quantum Verification & Benchmark Passport

## Thesis
Attach an evidence-backed passport to a quantum experiment, device run or algorithmic claim.

## Passport fields
- hardware/backend and calibration snapshot
- benchmark family and why it is appropriate
- circuit/compiler versions
- noise/mitigation assumptions
- repeatability statistics
- classical baseline
- uncertainty and failure modes

## Product value
Make quantum performance claims comparable and auditable instead of relying on isolated headline metrics.

## Buyers
Quantum hardware/software vendors, enterprise evaluators, research labs.
