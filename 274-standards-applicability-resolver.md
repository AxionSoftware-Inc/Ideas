# Standards Applicability Resolver

## Idea
Determine which standards, clauses and jurisdiction-specific requirements actually apply to a product, process or scientific workflow.

## Inputs
- product/system type
- intended use
- materials/processes
- market/jurisdiction
- lifecycle stage
- risk class
- referenced standards

## Outputs
- applicable clauses
- excluded clauses with reasons
- unresolved classification questions
- dependency chain of referenced standards

## Buyers
Engineering firms, compliance teams, certification bodies and regulated product companies.

## Moat
Context-sensitive rule applicability over a semantic standards graph.
