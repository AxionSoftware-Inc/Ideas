# Degradation-Cost Dispatch Optimizer

## Core idea
Optimize battery/storage dispatch using the true economic cost of degradation, uncertainty and future opportunity rather than energy price alone.

## Product
For every possible charge/discharge action, estimate immediate revenue, expected degradation cost, risk, remaining-life impact and optionality for future grid services. Produce a dispatch plan and explain the trade-off.

## Buyers
Grid-scale storage owners, aggregators, microgrids, commercial/industrial storage and fleet operators.

## Economic value
A dispatch policy can look profitable while consuming expensive battery life. The product turns physical degradation into a financial state variable.

## Strategic value
Connects scientific digital twins directly to money, which makes ROI measurable and creates recurring operational value instead of one-time engineering analysis.
