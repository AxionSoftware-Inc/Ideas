# Technology Readiness Evidence Engine

## Core idea
Replace subjective TRL labels with an evidence-backed readiness graph that separates scientific validity, prototype maturity, reproducibility, integration, manufacturing, reliability and operational readiness.

## Product
Every readiness claim is linked to supporting evidence and confidence. The engine identifies the weakest dimension, detects inflated readiness claims and recommends the next milestone that most increases commercial credibility.

## Buyers
Investors, grant programs, TTOs, corporate R&D and deep-tech startups.

## Economic value
A single TRL number hides very different risk profiles. Evidence-based readiness makes portfolio comparison and funding decisions more rational.

## Strategic value
Creates a common interface between Scientific Model Passports and investor/commercialization decisions.
