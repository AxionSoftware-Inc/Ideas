# Scientific Workflow Cost Profiler

## Thesis
Explain where time, money, memory, energy and experimental/compute budget are consumed across a scientific workflow.

## Profile dimensions
- solver/runtime hotspots
- repeated recomputation
- data movement
- over-precise calculations
- unnecessary high-fidelity stages
- expensive experiments with low information gain

## Output
Ranked optimization opportunities with estimated scientific impact and risk.

## Buyers
HPC users, industrial simulation, AI-for-science teams.

## MVP
Profile a simulation/optimization DAG and recommend cache, fidelity and hardware changes.
