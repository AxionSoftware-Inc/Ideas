# Scientific Knowledge Gap Mapper

## Goal
Identify where scientific knowledge is weak, contradictory or missing, and quantify which missing measurement would be most valuable.

The system maps a scientific domain by:
- property coverage
- uncertainty
- contradictory evidence
- untested regimes
- missing links between models and experiments
- sparse regions of parameter space

## Output
```text
High-value knowledge gap:
Material X fatigue behavior at 350-450 C
Evidence: sparse
Industrial relevance: high
Suggested next experiment: cyclic test at 400 C
Expected uncertainty reduction: 38%
```

## Business
R&D portfolio planning, materials discovery, pharma/chemistry experimentation and public research funding.

## Strategic value
Turns the knowledge graph into an active research-planning engine instead of a passive database.
