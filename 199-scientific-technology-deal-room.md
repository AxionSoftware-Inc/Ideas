# Scientific Technology Deal Room

## Core idea
A secure diligence workspace where a potential licensee/investor can inspect a technology through structured evidence instead of receiving a folder of PDFs.

## Product
Expose controlled views of performance claims, benchmarks, reproducibility, model/test provenance, IP dependencies, readiness gaps, pilot results and unresolved risks. Sensitive know-how can remain abstracted or permission-gated.

## Buyers
Deep-tech founders, TTOs, research institutes, investors and strategic acquirers.

## Economic value
Reduce information asymmetry and diligence friction while preserving trade-secret boundaries. Every buyer question can be linked to evidence and any new result updates the diligence graph automatically.

## Synergy
Private Scientific Knowledge Vault + Scientific Claim Auditor + Technology Readiness + IP Dependency Graph.
