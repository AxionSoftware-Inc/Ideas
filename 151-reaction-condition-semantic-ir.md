# Reaction Condition Semantic IR

## Core idea
Create a canonical machine-readable representation of a chemical reaction that includes more than a reaction SMILES: stoichiometry, concentrations, solvent, catalyst, addition order, temperature/time profile, pressure, mixing, atmosphere, light, workup, purification, analytical evidence and uncertainty.

## Why it matters
Reaction knowledge is often fragmented across prose, tables, supplementary information and instrument files. Models cannot reliably compare or reproduce experiments when crucial context is implicit.

## Product
Normalize literature and lab records into typed reaction objects that can be searched, simulated, optimized, reproduced and transferred across labs.

## Strategic value
This becomes the chemistry-domain extension of the Scientific Semantic IR and the foundation for reaction optimization, scale-up, reproducibility and autonomous labs.
