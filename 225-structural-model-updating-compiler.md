# Structural Model Updating Compiler

## Problem
Finite-element models drift from real structures as materials age, boundary conditions change and damage accumulates. Manual model updating is expert-heavy and can overfit noisy sensor data.

## Product
Assimilate modal, strain, displacement and environmental measurements into a structural model while preserving physically meaningful parameter bounds and uncertainty.

## Buyers
Bridge operators, wind-energy, civil infrastructure owners and structural consultants.

## Material value
Better damage diagnosis and lifecycle decisions from existing sensors.

## Moat
Physics-constrained updating with uncertainty and provenance rather than black-box fit.

## MVP
Update stiffness parameters in a simple beam/frame model from synthetic modal data.
