# Battery Fast-Charge Safety Envelope

## Core idea
Compile battery state, temperature, degradation mechanisms, uncertainty and charger constraints into a dynamic safe fast-charging envelope.

## Product
For the current cell/pack state, estimate allowable current/voltage trajectories subject to thermal, plating, degradation and uncertainty limits. Return both a recommended charging policy and safety margins.

## Buyers
EV OEMs, charging networks, battery manufacturers, robotics/drones and stationary storage.

## Economic value
Faster charging has customer value, but conservative limits waste capability while aggressive limits accelerate degradation or risk. A per-pack adaptive envelope monetizes better modeling directly through usable charge speed and lifetime.

## Synergy
Safety Envelope Compiler + Battery Twin + Uncertainty Engine + Optimal Control.
