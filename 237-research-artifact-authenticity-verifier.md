# Research Artifact Authenticity Verifier

## Problem
Datasets, images, model checkpoints and supplementary files can be copied, regenerated or altered without an obvious machine-readable distinction between original measurement, derived artifact and synthetic/AI-generated content.

## Product
Verify artifact identity, provenance, transformations, signatures and declared origin; flag unverifiable gaps and incompatible lineage claims.

## Buyers
Publishers, research institutions, regulated labs, data repositories and model registries.

## Strategic value
Provides a trust layer for increasingly AI-assisted science without pretending to infer truth from pixels alone.

## Moat
Cross-artifact provenance plus scientific process semantics.

## MVP
Verify a mixed bundle of raw, derived and synthetic artifacts from a reproducible experiment.
