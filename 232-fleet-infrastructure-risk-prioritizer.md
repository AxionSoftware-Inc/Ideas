# Fleet Infrastructure Risk Prioritizer

## Problem
Owners manage hundreds or thousands of assets with incomplete monitoring and limited inspection/repair budgets.

## Product
Combine structural condition, consequence of failure, model uncertainty, exposure and value-of-information to rank where inspection, sensors or repairs should go next.

## Buyers
Road/rail authorities, utilities, industrial asset owners and insurers.

## Material value
Direct budget optimization at portfolio scale.

## Moat
Asset-level physics/evidence feeding a portfolio decision model instead of condition scores alone.

## MVP
Rank intervention priorities across a synthetic fleet with uncertain deterioration states.
