# Seasonal Forecast-to-Farm Decision Compiler

## Idea
Translate probabilistic seasonal climate forecasts into farm decisions such as cultivar choice, planting window, irrigation strategy and input budget.

## Pipeline
`forecast ensemble + soil/crop models + economics + risk preference -> scenario simulation -> action policy`

## Outputs
- recommended decisions
- expected profit/yield distribution
- downside-risk exposure
- forecast value vs no-forecast baseline
- trigger points for strategy changes

## Buyers
Large farms, insurers, agronomy platforms and governments.

## Moat
Forecast uncertainty translated into operational/economic decisions.
