# Energy Asset Fleet Twin Orchestrator

## Core idea
Manage thousands of heterogeneous asset twins—batteries, inverters, transformers, HVAC, turbines or distributed energy resources—as a fleet rather than isolated dashboards.

## Product
Select the right fidelity per asset, share information across similar units, detect fleet-wide failure patterns, schedule recalibration/retraining and allocate scarce inspection/maintenance resources.

## Buyers
Utilities, storage fleets, renewable operators, industrial campuses, OEM service organizations.

## Economic value
A single high-fidelity twin may be too expensive to maintain for every asset. Fleet orchestration decides where detailed modeling is worth the compute/data/maintenance cost.

## Moat
Multi-fidelity orchestration + model passports + field provenance + R&D/maintenance portfolio optimization.
