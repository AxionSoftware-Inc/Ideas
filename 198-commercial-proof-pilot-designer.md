# Commercial Proof / Pilot Designer

## Core idea
Design the smallest pilot that proves what a buyer or licensee actually needs to believe, rather than repeating academic experiments at greater scale.

## Product
Given technology, target buyer and commercial objection set, identify critical claims, measurable acceptance criteria, baseline competitors, required environment and minimum evidence package. Optimize pilot cost/time against decision value.

## Buyers
Deep-tech startups, TTOs, corporate innovation teams and grant-funded commercialization programs.

## Economic value
A technically impressive demonstration can fail commercially if it does not answer buyer risk. This tool converts customer due-diligence questions into a scientific experiment/validation plan.

## Moat
Requirements-to-Physics + Optimal Experiment Design + Evidence Dossier + market capability model.
