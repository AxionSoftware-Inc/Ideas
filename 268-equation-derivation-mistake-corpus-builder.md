# Equation / Derivation Mistake Corpus Builder

## Idea
Build a structured corpus of real mathematical and physics mistakes from student/research derivations, tagged by semantic error type rather than surface form.

## Error taxonomy
- unit/dimension mismatch
- invalid algebraic transformation
- hidden assumption
- sign/convention error
- domain violation
- boundary/initial-condition misuse
- tensor/index mistake
- invalid approximation

## Uses
Adaptive tutoring, debugger benchmarks and scientific-AI evaluation.

## Buyers
Education platforms, universities and scientific-AI teams.

## Moat
Semantically labeled negative examples that are difficult to synthesize reliably.
