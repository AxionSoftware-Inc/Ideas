# Process Recipe Transferability Planner

## Core idea
Predict whether a validated manufacturing recipe can transfer to another machine, factory, material lot, geometry scale or supplier without full requalification.

## Product
Compare source and target capabilities, calibration state, dynamics, environment and material/process semantics. Separate invariant process intent from machine-specific settings, then propose parameter transformations and the minimum bridge tests required.

## Buyers
Multi-site manufacturers, contract manufacturers, additive manufacturing networks and industrial OEMs.

## Economic value
Moving a process between equipment/sites is often treated as a fresh development program. A transferability planner can reduce duplicated qualification work while making residual risk explicit.

## Moat
Semantic machine capability registry + process window + model validity + qualification graph.
