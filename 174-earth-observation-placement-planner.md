# Earth Observation Placement Planner

## Core idea
Choose where, when and what to measure to maximally reduce uncertainty in an Earth/environmental decision model.

## Product
Combine current model uncertainty, candidate sensors/satellites/stations, accessibility, cost and decision sensitivity. Recommend new weather stations, groundwater wells, field samples, remote-sensing acquisitions or monitoring frequency.

## Buyers
Environmental agencies, utilities, agriculture/water organizations, carbon-storage/geothermal projects and research programs.

## Economic value
Monitoring budgets are limited. Optimal Experiment Design applied spatially can identify measurements that actually change the decision instead of merely adding data volume.

## Moat
Scientific Data Valuation + Optimal Experiment Design + spatial model uncertainty.
