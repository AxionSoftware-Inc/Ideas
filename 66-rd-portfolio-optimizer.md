# R&D Portfolio Optimizer

## Goal
Allocate a finite research budget across experiments, simulations and projects based on expected scientific and commercial value.

## Inputs
- candidate experiments/projects
- cost and duration
- probability of technical success
- expected information gain
- dependency graph
- business value if successful
- shared equipment/resource constraints

## Output
A portfolio rather than a single recommendation:
- fund now
- delay until dependency is resolved
- cancel low-information work
- run cheap discriminating experiment first

## Strategic value
Connects scientific uncertainty to management decisions.

## Business
Industrial R&D organizations, national labs, materials/pharma programs and venture studios running multiple deep-tech projects.
