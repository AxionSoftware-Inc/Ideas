# Machine-Interpretable Standards Compiler

## Idea
Compile human-readable engineering standards into structured requirements, applicability conditions, definitions, tests and evidence obligations.

## Output objects
- requirement clauses
- normative definitions
- conditions/exceptions
- equations and limits
- referenced standards
- test methods
- evidence requirements
- version/provenance

## Buyers
Manufacturers, certification bodies, engineering software vendors and regulators.

## Moat
A verified semantic standards IR that can feed design, simulation, testing and compliance tools.
