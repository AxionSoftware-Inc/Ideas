# Water Network State & Leak Hypothesis Engine

## Idea
Fuse pressure, flow, topology, demand uncertainty and hydraulic models to maintain competing explanations for anomalies rather than one opaque leak score.

## Outputs
- likely leak zones
- sensor-fault alternative
- demand-model alternative
- evidence supporting each hypothesis
- best next measurement/valve action
- uncertainty-aware repair priority

## Buyers
Water utilities and industrial networks.

## MVP
District-metered water network with sparse pressure sensors.
