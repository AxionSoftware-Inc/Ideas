# Wafer Process Digital Twin Compiler

## Problem
Process engineers often maintain separate physics simulations, equipment recipes and wafer measurements. The digital twin becomes stale as the process changes.

## Product
Compile a semiconductor process flow into an executable wafer-level digital twin that links each unit process to geometry/material state, metrology and downstream device metrics.

`recipe -> process physics -> evolving wafer state -> virtual metrology -> measured update`

## Buyers
Logic/memory fabs, foundries, equipment makers and process-development teams.

## Material value
Reduce physical wafer experiments, shorten process-window exploration and expose which process steps dominate yield or device variation.

## Moat
Cross-step state representation plus calibrated process-to-device dependencies.

## MVP
Model a short 3–5 step synthetic process flow and calibrate it against metrology data.
