# Maintenance Action Value Engine

## Problem
Infrastructure maintenance decisions trade cost, risk, uncertainty, service disruption and future deterioration, but analyses are often fragmented across engineering and finance.

## Product
Evaluate candidate inspections, repairs, restrictions or replacement actions by expected lifecycle value under structural uncertainty.

## Buyers
Bridge/infrastructure owners, utilities, wind farms and asset managers.

## Material value
Directly optimize scarce maintenance budgets rather than merely predict condition.

## Moat
Physics-informed deterioration + uncertainty + intervention economics + evidence graph.

## MVP
Compare several maintenance actions on a synthetic degrading bridge component.
