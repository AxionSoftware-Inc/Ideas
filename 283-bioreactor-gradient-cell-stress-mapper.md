# Bioreactor Gradient-to-Cell Stress Mapper

## Goal
Translate CFD/process-scale gradients into the time-varying environment actually experienced by cells.

## Pipeline
`reactor CFD -> Lagrangian exposure histories -> oxygen/substrate/pH/shear cycles -> physiological response -> productivity risk`

## Value
Bridges reactor engineering and cell biology, the core scale-up gap in many bioprocesses.

## Outputs
- exposure distributions rather than average conditions
- stress hot spots
- phenotype-sensitive zones
- candidate scale-down waveforms
- reactor/design changes that reduce damaging heterogeneity

## Buyers
Bioreactor developers, biomanufacturers and process-development teams.

## MVP
Dissolved oxygen and glucose gradients for stirred-tank fermentation.
