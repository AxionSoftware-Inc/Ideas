# Subsurface Decision Uncertainty Engine

## Core idea
Convert uncertain subsurface models into explicit engineering decisions for geothermal, groundwater, carbon storage, mining or civil projects.

## Product
Given multiple plausible geological models and observations, calculate decision-relevant distributions such as target location, expected capacity, pressure response, monitoring value or failure probability. Identify which new data would most change the chosen action.

## Buyers
Geothermal developers, CCS projects, water utilities, mining and geotechnical engineering.

## Economic value
Subsurface uncertainty cannot usually be eliminated before action. The product optimizes decisions under ambiguity instead of pretending one inversion result is ground truth.

## Moat
Geophysical inversion + uncertainty + experiment/data valuation + decision optimization.
