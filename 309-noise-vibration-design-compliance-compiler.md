# Noise / Vibration Design Compliance Compiler

## Idea
Compile acoustic/vibration limits from standards and requirements into simulation/test checks linked directly to the design.

## Pipeline
`requirements -> measurable limits -> model/test definitions -> evidence -> compliance status`

## Change impact
When geometry, mount, material or operating condition changes, identify which NVH evidence is stale.

## Buyers
Automotive, appliances, industrial machinery, buildings and aerospace.

## MVP
One standardized vibration/noise requirement set.
