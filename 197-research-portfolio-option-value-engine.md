# Research Portfolio Option-Value Engine

## Core idea
Treat early scientific projects as portfolios of options rather than static ROI estimates. Value what each experiment, prototype or partnership teaches and which future commercialization paths it preserves.

## Product
Model technical uncertainty, milestone cost, branching outcomes, licensing/spinout/internal-product paths and shared dependencies across projects. Recommend where the next dollar of R&D creates the highest expected strategic option value.

## Buyers
Corporate R&D, government research programs, venture studios, universities and deep-tech holding companies.

## Economic value
The best early experiment may not maximize immediate performance; it may cheaply eliminate a major uncertainty or preserve several valuable future paths.

## Synergy
R&D Portfolio Optimizer + Scientific Data Valuation + Optimal Experiment Design + Technology Readiness Evidence.
