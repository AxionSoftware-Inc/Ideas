# Biomedical Model Evidence Passport

## Idea
Package a biomedical or physiological model with its validation evidence, uncertainty, intended-use claims, population coverage and known failure regimes.

## Why it matters
A model can be mathematically correct yet clinically inappropriate. The passport separates research-grade evidence from claims suitable for decision support.

## Core fields
- intended use
- patient/population scope
- calibration datasets
- validation cohorts
- uncertainty metrics
- transportability limits
- bias checks
- regulatory evidence links
- model/version provenance

## Buyers
Medical-device R&D, pharma modeling teams, hospitals, digital-health vendors and regulators.

## Moat
Evidence graph + model semantics + claim-to-validation traceability.
