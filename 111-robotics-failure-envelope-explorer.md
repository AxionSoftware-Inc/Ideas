# Robotics Failure-Envelope Explorer

## Thesis
Systematically search combinations of environment, uncertainty and component degradation that cause a robot/controller to violate requirements.

## Search dimensions
- friction and payload
- sensor dropout/bias
- latency/jitter
- actuator degradation
- obstacle geometry
- environmental disturbances
- model mismatch

## Output
A map of safe, degraded and failed regions plus minimal counterexample scenarios.

## Buyers
Autonomy QA, industrial robotics, safety engineering.

## MVP
Scenario search around a mobile robot/controller with reproducible failure capsules.
