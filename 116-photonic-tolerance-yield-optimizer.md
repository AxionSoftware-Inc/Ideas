# Photonic Tolerance & Yield Optimizer

## Thesis
Optimize a photonic design for production yield, not only nominal performance.

## Inputs
- process variation distributions
- lithography/etch bias
- temperature drift
- material uncertainty
- packaging/alignment tolerance

## Outputs
- expected yield
- dominant failure sensitivities
- robust geometry changes
- calibration requirements
- process-control priorities

## Buyers
Foundries and PIC product teams.

## MVP
Monte-Carlo/surrogate analysis of a passive PIC component with automated robust redesign.
