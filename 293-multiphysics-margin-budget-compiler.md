# Multi-Physics Margin Budget Compiler

## Problem
Safety/design margins are often distributed across thermal, structural, neutronic, material, measurement and model uncertainties.

## Product
Compile all contributors into a traceable margin budget and identify where engineering effort has the highest value.

## Outputs
- total margin distribution
- contributor ranking
- correlation handling
- conservative assumption audit
- evidence that can release unnecessary margin
- changes that consume margin

## Buyers
Nuclear, aerospace and other safety-critical engineering teams.

## MVP
Thermal/structural coupled safety limit.
