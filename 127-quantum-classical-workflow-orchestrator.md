# Quantum-Classical Workflow Orchestrator

## Thesis
Treat useful quantum applications as hybrid workflows, not isolated circuits.

## Orchestrate
- classical preprocessing
- circuit generation/compilation
- backend selection
- quantum execution
- mitigation
- classical optimization/postprocessing
- caching and repeated calibration

## Optimization targets
cost, latency, shot count, uncertainty, backend availability and classical fallback.

## Buyers
Quantum cloud/application platforms and enterprise R&D.

## MVP
A reproducible hybrid workflow for variational algorithms with backend-aware cost accounting and failure recovery.
