# Failure Data Scarcity Synthesizer

## Idea
Generate physically constrained synthetic degradation/failure trajectories when real failures are rare, while marking exactly what is simulated versus observed.

## Sources
Mechanistic models, accelerated tests, uncertainty distributions and sparse field histories.

## Rules
Synthetic data never silently becomes empirical evidence. Provenance and domain validity are first-class.

## Uses
Detector testing, maintenance-policy stress testing and experiment design.

## MVP
Rotating bearing or fatigue degradation model.
