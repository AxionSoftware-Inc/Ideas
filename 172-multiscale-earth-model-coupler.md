# Multiscale Earth Model Coupler

## Core idea
Semantically couple atmosphere, ocean, land, hydrology, wildfire, energy and local engineering/impact models across incompatible spatial grids, time scales and uncertainty representations.

## Product
- map variables and conventions;
- validate conservation/flux interfaces;
- reconcile resolutions and time stepping;
- quantify information lost during down/up-scaling;
- track which downstream indicators depend on which source models.

## Buyers
Climate services, research centers, insurers/risk teams, infrastructure planners and environmental engineering.

## Strategic value
Large Earth digital twins already generate high-resolution data. The commercial layer can be the trusted compiler from global models to local decision models.
