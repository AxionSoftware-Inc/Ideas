# Mathematical Notation Ontology & Translator

## Problem
The same symbol can mean radically different things across fields, while the same concept can be written in many incompatible notations.

## Product
A context-sensitive notation ontology that maps surface syntax to semantic mathematical identities.

## Examples
- physicists' vs mathematicians' Fourier transform conventions
- bra-ket vs matrix notation
- index vs coordinate-free tensors
- probability/statistics parameterizations
- sign conventions in curvature, metric signatures and transforms

## Capabilities
- detect notation/convention from context
- translate whole derivations, not isolated symbols
- preserve assumptions and sign choices
- flag ambiguous symbols before conversion

## Value
High-frequency painkiller for papers, textbooks, formalization, cross-domain collaboration and AI systems.

## Strategic role
Acts as the lexer/name-resolution layer above the Mathematical Object Registry and below the Semantic IR.
