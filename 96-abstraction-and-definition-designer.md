# Abstraction & Definition Designer

## Thesis
Major mathematical progress often comes from choosing the right definition or abstraction, not only proving another theorem.

## Product
Mine theorem/proof/model corpora for repeated structure and propose new definitions that compress many facts or expose common operators/properties.

## Signals
- repeated conjunctions of assumptions
- recurring proof fragments
- isomorphic theorem families
- common operator identities
- duplicated local structures
- clusters with similar dependency neighborhoods

## Evaluation
A proposed abstraction is scored by compression, theorem reuse, proof shortening, generalization power, conceptual coherence and whether it creates useful new statements.

## Validation
Generate API lemmas, migrate sample theorems to the abstraction, compare proof complexity, and formally verify equivalence to prior formulations where possible.

## Strategic value
This moves automated mathematics from theorem search toward **language design for mathematics**—potentially a much deeper discovery capability.
