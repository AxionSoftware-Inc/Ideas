# Contaminated-Site Decision-Grade Twin

## Idea
Maintain a living conceptual site model that explicitly links competing contaminant-transport/remediation hypotheses to evidence and trigger rules.

## Components
- geology/hydrogeology
- contaminant sources
- transport/reaction models
- monitoring wells
- remediation actions
- uncertainty and admissibility limits
- audit trail

## Value
Moves environmental twins from dashboards to defensible remediation decisions.

## MVP
Groundwater plume with pump-and-treat or bioremediation alternatives.
