# Yield-Loss Root Cause Graph

## Problem
Yield loss can emerge from interactions across hundreds of process steps, materials, tools and metrology signals. Correlation dashboards do not reliably distinguish root cause from downstream symptoms.

## Product
A causal/evidence graph connecting process excursions, tool history, wafer genealogy, metrology, defect signatures and electrical yield. Rank plausible causal chains and propose discriminating checks.

## Buyers
Fabs, yield-engineering teams and equipment vendors.

## Material value
Shorten time-to-root-cause and reduce wafer scrap during excursions.

## Moat
Process-physics priors + genealogy + evidence scoring + falsification tests.

## MVP
Use a synthetic or historical multi-step dataset and demonstrate faster localization of one injected process fault.
