# Metrology-to-Process Feedback Compiler

## Core idea
Automatically translate trusted inspection/metrology results into process-model updates and actionable manufacturing corrections.

## Product
A dimensional, surface, thermal or material measurement is mapped back to the process variables and physics that can explain it. Measurement uncertainty and calibration trust are carried into the correction decision.

## Example
Inspection detects a geometry bias. The system distinguishes true process drift from measurement-chain drift, identifies likely machine/process contributors and proposes a bounded correction or diagnostic test.

## Buyers
Precision manufacturing, additive manufacturing, coating, machining and automated production.

## Strategic value
Closes the digital thread loop: design -> process -> measurement -> evidence-aware process correction rather than one-way data collection.
