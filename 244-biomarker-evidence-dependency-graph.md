# Biomarker Evidence Dependency Graph

## Idea
Represent each biomarker claim as a dependency graph linking assay, preprocessing, cohort, endpoint, model, thresholds and published evidence.

## Why it matters
A biomarker may appear validated while depending on fragile preprocessing choices, narrow cohorts or unstable thresholds.

## Outputs
- claim provenance
- cohort coverage
- assay dependence
- threshold sensitivity
- contradictory evidence
- stale downstream conclusions after updates

## Buyers
Diagnostics companies, pharma translational teams, CROs and research hospitals.

## Moat
Fine-grained evidence graph from raw measurement to clinical claim.
