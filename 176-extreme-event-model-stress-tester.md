# Extreme-Event Model Stress Tester

## Core idea
Stress-test climate, hydrology, infrastructure-impact and environmental models specifically in rare/extreme regimes where ordinary average-error metrics are least informative.

## Product
Generate physically plausible extreme scenarios, compare multiple models/ensembles, search for regime failures, quantify tail uncertainty and identify which assumptions fail first.

## Buyers
Insurers, infrastructure owners, utilities, governments, renewable-energy and water-management organizations.

## Economic value
Most losses concentrate in tails. A model can score well overall yet be dangerously weak exactly where the decision matters most.

## Moat
Domain-of-validity search + model arbitration + uncertainty + failure-mode exploration applied to Earth-system decisions.
