# Extreme Load Scenario Composer

## Problem
Design checks often use prescribed load cases, but real failures can involve coupled wind, flood, thermal, seismic, traffic or impact conditions outside simple combinations.

## Product
Compose physically plausible extreme-load scenarios from hazard models, asset state and dependency rules, then prioritize the combinations most likely to expose structural weakness.

## Buyers
Civil infrastructure owners, insurers, engineering consultancies and resilience planners.

## Material value
More targeted stress testing and stronger resilience planning without brute-forcing every combination.

## Moat
Cross-hazard semantics + asset-specific vulnerability + uncertainty.

## MVP
Generate ranked multi-hazard load cases for a simple bridge or building model.
