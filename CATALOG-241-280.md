## Strategic pattern
Across all five domains, the valuable layer is not another predictor, dashboard or document repository. The recurring opportunity is to connect semantic objects, uncertainty, evidence, provenance, applicability and decision consequences so that scientific/engineering outputs become trustworthy and executable.
