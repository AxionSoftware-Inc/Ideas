# Scientific Audit Replay Engine

## Problem
Audits often verify documents rather than replaying the exact computational and measurement path that produced a scientific conclusion.

## Product
Given a claim or released result, reconstruct and replay its evidence chain: raw data, calibration state, transformations, software/model versions, parameters and acceptance criteria. Report every irreproducible or unverifiable step.

## Buyers
Regulated R&D, publishers, certification bodies, industrial QA and high-assurance research organizations.

## Material value
Turn expensive manual investigations into repeatable evidence-driven audits.

## Moat
Deep provenance plus executable scientific semantics and lockfiles.

## MVP
Replay one end-to-end experiment/simulation claim from a signed research capsule.
