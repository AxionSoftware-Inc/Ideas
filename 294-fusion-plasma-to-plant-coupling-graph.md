# Fusion Plasma-to-Plant Coupling Graph

## Idea
Represent dependencies from plasma scenarios through magnets, heating, vacuum, blanket, thermal systems, power conversion, tritium and maintenance constraints.

## Why
Fusion optimization is frequently split across specialist models. A gain in plasma performance can create unacceptable plant-level loads or economics.

## Capabilities
- semantic interfaces between model domains
- unit/frame/time-scale checks
- change-impact propagation
- cross-domain constraint queries
- scenario provenance

## Long-term value
A system-of-systems knowledge layer for fusion plant design.

## MVP
Plasma pulse -> heat loads -> cooling/power subsystem coupling.
