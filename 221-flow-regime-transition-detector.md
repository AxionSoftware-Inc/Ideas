# Flow Regime Transition Detector

## Problem
A simulation or reduced model may remain numerically stable while the real flow crosses into a different regime where its assumptions fail.

## Product
Track nondimensional groups, stability indicators, empirical evidence and model residuals to warn when laminar/turbulent, attached/separated, incompressible/compressible or other regime transitions approach.

## Buyers
Aerospace, process engineering, HVAC, turbomachinery and fluid-system digital twins.

## Material value
Prevent extrapolation beyond model validity and trigger higher-fidelity analysis only when needed.

## Moat
Regime semantics linked to validity evidence and runtime monitoring.

## MVP
Implement transition warnings for several canonical flow regimes.
