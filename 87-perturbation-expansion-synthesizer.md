# Perturbation Expansion Synthesizer

## Thesis
Perturbation theory is systematic enough to automate much more aggressively than current notebook workflows.

## Product
Given a model and one or more small parameters, generate controlled perturbative expansions and track validity.

## Capabilities
- identify candidate small parameters
- choose expansion variables/scales
- derive orders automatically
- remove secular terms using appropriate methods
- construct multiple-scales or matched-asymptotic expansions
- estimate truncation error
- compare against numerical reference solutions

## Output
Human-readable derivation + symbolic expansion + executable approximation + validity map.

## Value
Useful across mechanics, quantum physics, waves, fluids, controls and engineering where full numerical solves are expensive or hide intuition.

## Moat
Integrates semantic assumptions, asymptotic regimes, operator algebra and verification rather than acting as a generic series expander.
