# Optical System Co-Design Engine

## Thesis
Optimize optical hardware and downstream computation/control together instead of treating optics as a fixed front end.

## Examples
- lens/metasurface + reconstruction algorithm
- photonic accelerator + electronic calibration
- sensor optics + inference model

## Objective
Search system-level tradeoffs among optical performance, fabrication complexity, compute cost, calibration and robustness.

## Buyers
Imaging, AR/VR, sensing, photonic computing.

## Moat
Shared semantic objective graph spanning Maxwell-level device models and system/application metrics.
