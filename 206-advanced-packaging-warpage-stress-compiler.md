# Advanced Packaging Warpage & Stress Compiler

## Problem
Chiplet and advanced-package reliability depends on interacting material properties, cure history, moisture, geometry, thermal cycles, residual stress and warpage. These inputs are often managed across disconnected tools.

## Product
Compile package stack-up + material/process history into a coupled thermo-mechanical model with uncertainty, dominant contributors and manufacturability limits.

## Buyers
OSATs, chiplet teams, packaging R&D, materials suppliers and reliability groups.

## Material value
Reduce package iterations, warpage failures and reliability surprises before expensive builds.

## Moat
Process-history-aware material models plus evidence-linked property uncertainty.

## MVP
Model a simplified multilayer package through cure + cool-down and rank dominant warpage contributors.
