# Virtual Cohort Generator & Coverage Auditor

## Idea
Generate biologically constrained virtual cohorts for simulation studies, then audit whether they adequately cover the intended population and risk factors.

## Core checks
- parameter-space coverage
- demographic/physiological strata
- rare-risk regimes
- correlation realism
- synthetic-to-real distribution gaps
- sensitivity of conclusions to cohort design

## Buyers
Pharma modeling, device simulation, in-silico trial teams and regulators.

## Moat
Mechanistic constraints + uncertainty + population coverage evidence, not generic synthetic tabular data.
