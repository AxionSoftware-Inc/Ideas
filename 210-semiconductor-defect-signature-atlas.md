# Semiconductor Defect Signature Atlas

## Problem
Defect images and electrical failure signatures are often classified locally, but the link from observed signature to plausible process mechanism and evidence is weak.

## Product
A structured atlas mapping visual/electrical defect signatures to candidate mechanisms, process steps, tool histories, distinguishing tests and confidence.

## Buyers
Yield engineering, failure analysis, equipment vendors and fabs.

## Material value
Faster excursion triage and less expert time spent rediscovering known patterns.

## Moat
Mechanism-grounded signatures linked to process context rather than image labels alone.

## MVP
Build a small curated atlas for a single defect family with synthetic examples and diagnostic decision trees.
