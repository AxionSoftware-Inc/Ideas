# Scientific Pipeline Supply-Chain Scanner

## Problem
Research results depend on packages, containers, models, datasets, plugins and external binaries. Traditional vulnerability scanners do not understand which scientific conclusions depend on which components.

## Product
Build a dependency/SBOM graph for a scientific workflow, score software/data/model supply-chain risk and identify which published results are affected by a vulnerable or changed dependency.

## Buyers
Research institutes, HPC centers, regulated R&D and publishers.

## Material value
Prioritize security work by scientific impact rather than package count.

## Moat
Scientific result-to-dependency impact mapping plus reproducibility metadata.

## MVP
Scan a Python/Jupyter research project and map dependencies to generated figures/results.
