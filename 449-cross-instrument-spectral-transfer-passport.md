# Cross-Instrument Spectral Transfer Passport

Determine whether a calibration/model from one spectrometer can be trusted on another.

Outputs:
- instrument-response mismatch
- required transfer samples
- selected transfer method
- residual bias/variance
- post-transfer uncertainty
- drift-monitoring requirements
- transfer validity envelope

The goal is not only to transfer a model, but to certify the limits of that transfer.
