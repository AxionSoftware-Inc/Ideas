# Hardware-Aware Quantum Portability Planner

## Thesis
Given one semantic quantum workload, determine how it should change across hardware backends instead of treating portability as syntax conversion.

## Consider
- connectivity/topology
- native gates
- dynamic-circuit support
- measurement/reset latency
- noise profiles
- qubit quality and drift
- queue/runtime constraints

## Output
Backend-specific compiled candidates plus predicted cost/error tradeoffs and semantic-equivalence checks.

## Buyers
Quantum software teams and multi-provider users.
