# Manufacturing Defect Root-Cause Engine

## Core idea
Infer why a manufactured part/process failed by combining process physics, machine logs, sensor data, material lots, environmental data, inspection results and historical production evidence.

## Product
Maintain competing causal hypotheses rather than one opaque anomaly score. Recommend discriminating inspections or process experiments and estimate which upstream variables most likely created the observed defect morphology.

## Buyers
Advanced manufacturing, coatings, battery production, semiconductor/process lines, composites and machining.

## Economic value
Reduce scrap and troubleshooting time by turning quality failures into structured evidence that improves future process models.

## Moat
Process-Structure-Property Graph + Missing Physics Diagnoser + metrology-aware sensor provenance.
