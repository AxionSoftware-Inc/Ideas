# Fab Qualification Evidence Compiler

## Problem
Process and equipment qualification evidence is distributed across experiments, SPC records, metrology, reports and customer sign-off documents.

## Product
Compile qualification requirements into a live evidence graph showing what is proven, missing, stale or invalidated by process changes.

## Buyers
Fabs, equipment suppliers, quality/reliability teams and customers qualifying suppliers.

## Material value
Shorter qualification cycles, fewer duplicate tests and clearer audit readiness.

## Moat
Requirement-to-measurement-to-process traceability with semantic change impact.

## MVP
Support one equipment/process qualification workflow with machine-readable evidence status.
