# Mathematical Frontier Mapper

## Problem
A field's frontier is scattered across papers, open conjectures, partially formalized results, unresolved dependencies and duplicated problem formulations.

## Product
Construct a live map of a mathematical area's research frontier.

## Nodes
- known theorems
- open conjectures
- conditional results
- unresolved lemmas
- counterexamples
- computational evidence
- active proof attempts
- formalization status

## Ranking
Estimate foundational importance, dependency centrality, tractability, evidence strength, novelty and expected downstream impact.

## Value
Helps researchers and AI systems decide what to work on next rather than merely answering isolated questions.

## Strategic role
Combines theorem graphs, conjecture value, knowledge-gap mapping, formal-library coverage and proof-cost profiling into a research navigation product.

## Business
Research labs, universities, grant programs and AI-math organizations could use private frontier maps for portfolio planning.
