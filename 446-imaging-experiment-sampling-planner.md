# Imaging Experiment & Sampling Planner

Choose image count, fields of view, z-spacing, time points, magnification and replicate structure for a target scientific precision.

Inputs:
spatial heterogeneity, effect size, acquisition time, phototoxicity, instrument capacity and desired uncertainty.

Outputs:
- minimum defensible imaging plan
- marginal value of extra images
- underpowered sampling warnings
- acquisition-cost estimate

**Value:** stop oversampling easy regions while missing biological/physical heterogeneity.
