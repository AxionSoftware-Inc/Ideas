# Measurement Trust Passport

## Core idea
Attach a compact machine-readable trust passport to every important measurement result: traceability status, uncertainty, calibration freshness, environmental validity, data integrity and decision suitability.

## Example
A result is not merely `12.402 mm`; it carries:
- expanded uncertainty;
- traceability chain status;
- instrument/calibration identity;
- environmental range compliance;
- procedure version;
- anomaly flags;
- intended-use suitability.

## Product value
Downstream software can reject, accept or conditionally use measurements without reading PDFs or manually interpreting certificates.

## Buyers
Manufacturing QA, digital twins, autonomous labs, inspection systems, regulated test environments.

## Strategic value
This is the measurement equivalent of the Model Passport and Proof Provenance Certificate, enabling a common trust architecture across physical measurements, simulations and formal results.
