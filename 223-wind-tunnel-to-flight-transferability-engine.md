# Wind-Tunnel-to-Flight Transferability Engine

## Problem
Wind-tunnel results require corrections for scale, blockage, support interference, Reynolds/Mach mismatch and instrumentation before they can support flight decisions.

## Product
Represent tunnel/test conditions semantically and compute which results transfer to target flight regimes, with corrections, uncertainty and unsupported extrapolations explicitly marked.

## Buyers
Aerospace OEMs, drone companies, wind-tunnel facilities and research labs.

## Material value
Reduce duplicate testing and improve confidence in ground-to-flight correlation.

## Moat
Evidence-linked scaling/correction knowledge across facilities and configurations.

## MVP
Support one low-speed airfoil/tunnel scenario and produce a transferability report.
