# Environmental Model Admissibility Gate

## Problem
A calibrated model can look excellent while being used for a decision outside the conditions supported by its data or physics.

## Product
Before a regulatory or operational use, test whether the model is admissible for that specific claim.

## Checks
- spatial/temporal extrapolation
- forcing regime
- parameter identifiability
- structural uncertainty
- observation coverage
- alternative models
- decision sensitivity

## Output
USE / USE WITH MARGIN / REVALIDATE / DO NOT USE.

## MVP
Groundwater or watershed forecasting model.
