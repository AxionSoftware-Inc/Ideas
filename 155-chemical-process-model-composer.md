# Chemical Process Model Composer

## Core idea
Assemble chemical-process digital twins from reusable unit-operation, kinetics, thermodynamics, transport and control models through semantic interfaces.

## Product
Given a flowsheet/process description, find compatible models, reconcile units/assumptions, identify missing parameters, calibrate against plant/lab data and produce an executable model graph.

## Buyers
Chemical engineering, process development, pharma manufacturing, energy/process industries.

## Why valuable
Process models are often trapped in vendor files, custom scripts and engineer-specific conventions. A semantic composer reduces repeated model rebuilding and makes model provenance explicit.

## Moat
A registry of typed chemical process models plus automatic model compatibility and calibration. It extends the generic Scientific Model ABI into chemistry/process engineering.
