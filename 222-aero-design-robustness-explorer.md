# Aero Design Robustness Explorer

## Problem
A design optimized at one nominal condition can degrade badly under manufacturing tolerance, atmospheric variation, roughness, contamination or off-design operation.

## Product
Explore aerodynamic performance across uncertain geometry, operating conditions and model uncertainty; identify designs with stable performance rather than only peak nominal performance.

## Buyers
Aerospace, drones, turbomachinery, automotive aero and wind-energy teams.

## Material value
Reduce redesign and field-performance surprises.

## Moat
Joint geometry/process/operating uncertainty with multi-fidelity simulation orchestration.

## MVP
Robustly rank several airfoil designs across Reynolds number and geometric perturbations.
