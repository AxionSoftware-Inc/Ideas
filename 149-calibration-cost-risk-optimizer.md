# Calibration Cost / Risk Optimizer

## Core idea
Optimize an organization's calibration strategy across thousands of instruments under budget, downtime, traceability and quality-risk constraints.

## Inputs
Instrument criticality, historical drift, calibration price, shipping/downtime, redundancy, product tolerance, uncertainty contribution and downstream impact.

## Outputs
A portfolio-level schedule answering where calibration money has the highest risk-reduction value, which instruments can safely extend intervals, which require redundancy, and where replacement is cheaper than repeated calibration.

## Buyers
Factories, utilities, airlines, laboratories, pharma/process plants and metrology service providers.

## Economic thesis
Calibration is usually managed per instrument. The higher-value product treats the whole measurement fleet as an optimization problem tied to production and quality risk.
