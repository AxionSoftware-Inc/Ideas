# Experiment Measurement Chain Autoconfigurator

## Problem
Researchers often assemble sensors, amplifiers, DAQs, filters, sampling rates and calibration chains manually, creating avoidable range, noise, aliasing and uncertainty errors.

## Product
Given a target quantity and required accuracy/bandwidth, synthesize a valid measurement chain from available instruments and verify the full uncertainty/noise budget.

## Buyers
R&D labs, test engineering, universities and industrial validation teams.

## Material value
Reduce setup time, bad experiments and over-specified hardware purchases.

## Moat
Physics-aware component compatibility plus end-to-end uncertainty reasoning.

## MVP
Support voltage/temperature/force measurements with a small hardware catalog.
