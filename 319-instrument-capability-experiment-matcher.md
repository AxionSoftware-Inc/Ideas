# Instrument Capability-to-Experiment Matcher

## Idea
Match a scientific question and required measurement specification to instruments/facilities by actual capability, not vendor category names.

## Semantic capability
Resolution, sensitivity, range, sample constraints, environment, throughput, calibration traceability, uncertainty and available methods.

## Output
- compatible instruments/facilities
- why they qualify
- missing capability
- expected cost/wait
- cheaper acceptable alternatives

## Strategic value
Connects the Instrument Capability Registry to a marketplace/router.

## MVP
Microscopy or spectroscopy capability ontology.
