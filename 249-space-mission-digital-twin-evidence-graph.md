# Space Mission Digital Twin Evidence Graph

## Idea
Connect spacecraft/robot digital-twin states and predictions to the evidence that justifies them across design, qualification, launch and operations.

## Graph links
- design assumptions
- subsystem models
- ground tests
- environmental qualification
- telemetry
- calibration
- anomaly history
- software/configuration versions
- operational decisions

## Buyers
Spacecraft integrators, mission operations teams and space robotics programs.

## Moat
Lifecycle-spanning evidence graph that survives model and configuration changes.
