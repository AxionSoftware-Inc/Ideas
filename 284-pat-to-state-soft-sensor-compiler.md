# PAT-to-State Soft Sensor Compiler

## Problem
Many biologically important states are difficult or expensive to measure continuously.

## Product
Given available process analytical technology (PAT), mechanistic models and desired hidden states, synthesize and validate virtual sensors.

## Examples
Infer biomass, metabolic state, substrate uptake, viable cell density or product quality from spectroscopy, off-gas, pH, DO and feed signals.

## Trust layer
Every estimate carries observability, calibration range, uncertainty, drift status and evidence provenance.

## Business value
Reduces physical sampling, enables tighter control and upgrades existing bioreactors without replacing hardware.

## MVP
One fermentation process with off-gas + DO + feed based biomass/uptake estimation.
