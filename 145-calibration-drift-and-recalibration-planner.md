# Calibration Drift & Recalibration Planner

## Core idea
Replace fixed calendar-based recalibration intervals with evidence-based calibration planning using historical drift, usage intensity, environment, device family behavior and required decision risk.

## Product
For each instrument, estimate drift probability and the cost/risk trade-off of recalibrating now versus later. Recommend individualized intervals and flag instruments whose behavior changes unexpectedly.

## Outputs
- predicted drift trajectory;
- probability of leaving tolerance before next calibration;
- recommended recalibration date;
- expected downtime/cost;
- affected downstream measurements if the instrument fails retrospectively.

## Buyers
Large calibration fleets, factories, utilities, laboratories, hospitals and field-service organizations.

## Business value
Reduce unnecessary calibration while lowering the probability of invalid measurements and costly retrospective product review.
