# Fleet Failure-Mechanism Transferability Engine

## Problem
Failure knowledge learned on one asset/site/population is often transferred blindly to another.

## Product
Determine whether a degradation/failure mechanism and prognostic model are transferable across fleets.

## Compare
Design revision, material, load spectrum, environment, maintenance history, sensors and operating policy.

## Output
- transferable evidence
- invalid evidence
- required adaptation
- minimum new validation data

## Strategic value
Makes sparse failure data much more reusable without pretending populations are identical.

## MVP
Two related machine or battery fleets.
