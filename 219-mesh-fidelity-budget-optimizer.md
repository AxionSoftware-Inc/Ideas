# Mesh / Fidelity Budget Optimizer

## Problem
Engineers often overspend compute uniformly even though only some regions/outputs need high spatial or temporal fidelity.

## Product
Allocate mesh density, timestep and model fidelity against a target error budget. Predict where refinement buys meaningful accuracy and where it does not.

## Buyers
CFD/FEM teams, aerospace, automotive and simulation consultancies.

## Material value
Lower compute cost and shorten design loops while preserving decision accuracy.

## Moat
Goal-oriented error budgeting connected to solver benchmarks and physical regimes.

## MVP
Optimize mesh/refinement for one canonical CFD benchmark and target quantity.
