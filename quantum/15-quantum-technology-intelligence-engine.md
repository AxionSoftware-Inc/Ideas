# Quantum Technology Intelligence Engine

## Problem
Quantum hardware claims are difficult to compare because vendors publish different metrics, roadmaps, architectures and assumptions. Investors, governments and enterprises struggle to convert those claims into realistic capability forecasts.

## Product
A structured intelligence engine that continuously normalizes public hardware, QEC, architecture and roadmap data into comparable models and links those models to algorithm/resource requirements.

## Core flow
Vendor claims + papers + benchmarks + hardware specs -> normalized evidence graph -> capability model -> feasibility scenarios -> confidence-rated forecast.

## Buyers
Governments, defense, investors, consulting firms, enterprise strategy teams, research organizations and quantum vendors.

## Moat
Historical evidence graph, normalization ontology, claim-vs-result tracking, hardware/QEC model library and integration with the resource/economics engine.

## MVP
Track a small set of major hardware vendors and produce evidence-linked comparisons for logical qubit progress, error rates, cycle times and projected workload feasibility.

## Strategic value
This can become the intelligence layer around the rest of the quantum stack: it converts fragmented announcements into decision-grade engineering scenarios rather than a news feed.
