# Quantum Result Certification Engine

## Problem
When quantum hardware reaches regimes that are difficult to simulate classically, users still need evidence that a result is trustworthy. A raw result plus vendor confidence metrics is not enough for science, finance, chemistry or regulated decisions.

## Product
A cross-checking trust layer that combines cross-device execution, randomized verification, partial classical simulation, symmetry/conservation checks, mitigation validation, statistical consistency and provenance into a confidence certificate.

## Buyers
Pharma, materials companies, finance, defense, national labs, research institutions and quantum cloud platforms.

## Moat
Verification protocols, workload-specific invariants, cross-backend comparison, confidence scoring and tamper-evident evidence packages.

## MVP
Start with workloads small enough for partial classical validation. Compare hardware/simulator outputs and generate a machine-readable trust report explaining which checks passed and failed.

## Strategic value
Quantum advantage creates a verification paradox: the more classically difficult the result is, the harder independent validation becomes. A trusted certification layer becomes more valuable as hardware improves.
