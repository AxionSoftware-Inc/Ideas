# Quantum Hardware Benchmark & Procurement Intelligence

## Problem
Qubit counts, vendor-specific quality metrics, fidelities, queue times and pricing are difficult to compare directly. Buyers need workload-specific evidence, not marketing numbers.

## Product
A neutral benchmarking and procurement layer that runs or ingests standardized workloads across providers and compares accuracy, runtime, queue delay, shot cost, reproducibility, calibration sensitivity and total cost-to-answer.

## Buyers
Enterprises, governments, research labs, HPC centers, cloud brokers and quantum application teams.

## Moat
Cross-vendor workload corpus, normalized metrics, historical hardware performance data, reproducibility scoring and recommendation models tied to actual workloads.

## MVP
Support a small provider set plus simulators. Run identical benchmark families and produce a normalized report for one workload category.

## Strategic value
As the hardware market fragments, buyers will need an independent layer answering 'which machine should run this workload, at what expected cost and confidence?'.
