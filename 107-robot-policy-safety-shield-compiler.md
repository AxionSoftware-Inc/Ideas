# Robot Policy Safety-Shield Compiler

## Thesis
Compile formal safety requirements and plant limits into an independent runtime layer that can constrain or override learned/autonomous policies.

## Architecture
`AI/learned policy -> proposed action -> verified safety shield -> actuator`

## Value
Keep high-performance AI control separate from a simpler auditable assurance layer.

## Evidence
Modern robotics research increasingly explores formally verifiable independent safety oversight for learned/autonomous policies.

## Buyers
Safety-critical robotics, inspection, warehouses, industrial autonomy.

## MVP
Mobile robot with obstacle/velocity/geofence constraints and formally specified runtime interventions.
