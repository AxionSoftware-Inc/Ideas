# Fabrication-Aware Photonics Compiler

## Thesis
Compile ideal photonic designs into manufacturable designs under real foundry rules, process corners and tolerance distributions.

## Pipeline
`optical objective -> inverse/parametric design -> foundry constraints -> process-corner simulation -> tolerance repair -> layout-ready candidate`

## Why now
Photonics inverse design is moving from university demonstrations toward scalable systems and commercial foundries; manufacturability is becoming part of the optimization problem.

## Buyers
PIC startups, foundries, photonic EDA.

## MVP
Waveguide bend/coupler optimization with minimum-feature, etch-bias and width-variation constraints.
