# Composite Cure Window & Residual-Stress Compiler

Couple thermo-chemo-mechanical cure models with autoclave/tool thermal history, resin kinetics and part geometry.

Outputs:
- degree-of-cure distribution
- exotherm/hotspot risk
- residual stress/distortion
- void/consolidation risk
- robust cure window
- cycle changes with evidence impact

**MVP:** autoclave-cured thermoset component.
