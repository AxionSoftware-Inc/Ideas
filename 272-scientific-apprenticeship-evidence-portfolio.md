# Scientific Apprenticeship Evidence Portfolio

## Idea
Convert real project work into a structured portfolio of demonstrated scientific capabilities.

## Evidence objects
- derivations
- model validation
- code/results
- experiment plans
- uncertainty analyses
- proof certificates
- peer/supervisor review
- reproducibility records

## Value
Lets employers or research groups inspect what a candidate can actually do rather than infer skill from credentials alone.

## Buyers
Universities, research programs, technical employers and fellowship programs.

## Moat
Direct linkage between competency graph and verifiable scientific artifacts.
