# Coordinate & Representation Compiler

## Problem
Many hard derivations become easy in the right representation: Cartesian vs curvilinear coordinates, real vs Fourier space, matrix vs operator form, Eulerian vs Lagrangian descriptions, primal vs dual formulations.

## Product
Automatically propose and apply mathematically valid representation changes, carrying units, assumptions, boundary conditions and conventions with them.

## Capabilities
- coordinate transforms with Jacobians/metrics
- Fourier/Laplace/spectral transforms
- basis changes and diagonalization
- operator ↔ matrix/tensor forms
- weak/strong formulation conversion
- dimensionality reduction under symmetry

## Output
Transformed model + inverse mapping + changed assumptions/BCs + simplification score + verification report.

## Value
A representation compiler can unlock simpler solvers, faster simulation and shorter proofs without changing the underlying physics.
