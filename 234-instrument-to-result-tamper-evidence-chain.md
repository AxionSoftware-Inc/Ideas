# Instrument-to-Result Tamper-Evidence Chain

## Problem
Even if final files are signed, a scientific result can be compromised earlier through altered instrument configuration, calibration state, firmware, acquisition settings or preprocessing.

## Product
Create an end-to-end evidence chain from instrument identity/configuration and calibration through raw acquisition to final claim, with integrity checks at each boundary.

## Buyers
High-assurance labs, regulated test organizations, industrial R&D and autonomous facilities.

## Material value
Detect where trust was lost and reduce expensive repeat testing after an integrity incident.

## Moat
Instrument-aware provenance tied to measurement semantics and calibration state.

## MVP
Secure one simulated instrument-to-analysis pipeline with signed state snapshots.
