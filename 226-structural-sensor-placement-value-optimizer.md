# Structural Sensor Placement Value Optimizer

## Problem
Infrastructure monitoring budgets are limited, but sensor placement is often based on heuristics rather than quantified decision value.

## Product
Choose sensor types and locations that maximize expected reduction in uncertainty about damage, remaining life or maintenance decisions under a fixed budget.

## Buyers
Bridge authorities, infrastructure operators, wind farms and SHM vendors.

## Material value
Fewer sensors for the same decision quality or better monitoring from the same budget.

## Moat
Decision-aware information value tied to structural models and failure hypotheses.

## MVP
Optimize accelerometer/strain-gauge placement on a simple bridge or frame model.
