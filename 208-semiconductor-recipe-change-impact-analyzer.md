# Semiconductor Recipe Change-Impact Analyzer

## Problem
Small process-recipe changes can propagate through downstream geometry, stress, electrical performance and qualification requirements.

## Product
Compare two recipe/process versions semantically and estimate affected downstream steps, metrology, device metrics, tests and requalification scope.

## Buyers
Fabs, process-integration teams and equipment vendors.

## Material value
Avoid unnecessary full requalification while catching hidden downstream risk.

## Moat
A process dependency graph grounded in physical state transitions rather than text diffs.

## MVP
Track change impact across a simplified multi-step process graph.
