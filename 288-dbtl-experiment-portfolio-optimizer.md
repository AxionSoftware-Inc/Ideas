# DBTL Experiment Portfolio Optimizer

## Idea
Choose a portfolio of Design-Build-Test-Learn experiments for total information and commercial value, not one Bayesian-optimization point at a time.

## Objective
Balance novelty, uncertainty reduction, expected performance, diversity, experiment cost, equipment capacity and scale-up relevance.

## Outputs
- ranked experiment batch
- expected information gain
- redundancy warnings
- capacity-aware schedule
- value of each measurement
- stop/exploit/explore recommendation

## Buyers
Synthetic-biology platforms and automated labs.

## Moat
Combines semantic experiment identity, causal evidence and downstream scale-up/economic value.

## MVP
Plate/bioreactor strain-screening campaign.
