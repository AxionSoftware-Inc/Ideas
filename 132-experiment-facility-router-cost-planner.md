# Experiment Facility Router & Cost Planner

## Thesis
Given an experiment plan, determine which external facilities can execute each step and optimize the route across cost, lead time, capability, geography, sample handling and data quality.

## Workflow
`experiment DAG -> required capabilities -> facility matching -> logistics/access constraints -> cost/time plan`

## Value
Turns fragmented research infrastructure into a schedulable supply chain.

## Buyers
Startups without their own labs, universities, corporate R&D and research consortia.

## Strategic note
Pairs naturally with the Research Infrastructure Capability Marketplace and Autonomous Lab Protocol Compiler.
