# Climate Scenario Dependency Auditor

## Core idea
Audit an adaptation, risk or investment conclusion to show exactly how strongly it depends on emissions scenario, climate model, ensemble member, downscaling, impact model and socioeconomic assumptions.

## Product
Perturb or swap upstream assumptions and report which conclusions remain robust, which reverse and which are unsupported outside narrow scenario choices.

## Buyers
Infrastructure planning, insurers, banks, governments, energy developers and climate-service providers.

## Value
A polished risk number may hide deep scenario dependence. This tool separates robust decisions from conclusions that are artifacts of one modeling chain.

## Strategic value
A decision-facing extension of Scientific Claim Auditor and Provenance Graph for climate risk.
