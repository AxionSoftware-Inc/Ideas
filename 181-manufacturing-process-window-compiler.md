# Manufacturing Process Window Compiler

## Core idea
Compile material, machine, geometry, process physics, quality requirements and uncertainty into a validated process window rather than a single nominal recipe.

## Product
Map regions of parameter space as validated, marginal, unknown or unsafe; identify dominant failure mechanisms and automatically design qualification experiments near the boundaries that matter.

## Buyers
Additive manufacturing, coating, machining, semiconductor/process industries, composites and advanced manufacturing.

## Economic value
A narrow recipe is fragile and requalification is expensive. A quantified process window improves robustness, transferability and change control.

## Moat
Physics-aware design of experiments + domain-of-validity mapping + qualification evidence graph.
