# Material Qualification Planner

## Problem
Finding a promising material is easier than qualifying it for a real product.

## Product
Given a material, application and target standard, generate the minimum defensible qualification program.

Output can include:
- required tests and environmental conditions
- sample counts and statistical confidence targets
- accelerated-aging plans
- missing evidence
- simulation evidence that can reduce physical testing
- acceptance criteria
- traceability package

## Value
Qualification can consume months of engineering effort. The planner turns standards, model validity, uncertainty and test evidence into an optimized validation campaign.

## Business
Aerospace, automotive, medical devices, energy, electronics and other regulated or safety-critical manufacturing.
