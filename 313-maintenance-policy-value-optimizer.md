# Maintenance Policy Value Optimizer

## Goal
Translate health estimates into economic maintenance actions.

## Options
Run, inspect, derate, repair, replace or gather more data.

## Engine
`failure probability + consequence + downtime + spares + labor + uncertainty -> expected lifecycle value`

## Outputs
- action by asset
- expected cost/risk
- value of waiting
- value of another inspection
- policy sensitivity

## Buyers
Industrial fleets, utilities, transportation and manufacturing.

## MVP
Small fleet with known failure-cost structure.
