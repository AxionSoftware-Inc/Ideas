# Evidence Contradiction Sentinel

## Problem
New measurements, papers or recalibrations can contradict assumptions or parameter values that many downstream models and conclusions still depend on.

## Product
Continuously compare new evidence against the scientific knowledge/provenance graph and surface contradictions with an impact map of affected models, claims, reports and decisions.

## Buyers
R&D organizations, publishers, digital-twin operators and scientific knowledge platforms.

## Material value
Prevent stale conclusions from surviving after their evidence base changes.

## Moat
Semantic dependency and validity graph spanning evidence to decisions.

## MVP
Inject a changed property/calibration into a small model graph and identify all conclusions requiring review.
