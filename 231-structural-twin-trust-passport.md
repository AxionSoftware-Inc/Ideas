# Structural Twin Trust Passport

## Problem
A bridge/building digital twin may update in real time yet still have poorly quantified model-form error, sensor health or calibration validity.

## Product
Issue a live trust passport containing calibration status, sensor coverage, residuals, uncertainty, model validity, last verified state and decisions the twin is qualified to support.

## Buyers
Infrastructure owners, digital-twin vendors, engineering firms and regulators.

## Material value
Prevent high-consequence decisions from an unqualified twin and make assurance auditable.

## Moat
Dynamic trust status tied to physical evidence rather than a one-time validation report.

## MVP
Wrap a simple SHM twin and vary trust as sensors/model residuals degrade.
