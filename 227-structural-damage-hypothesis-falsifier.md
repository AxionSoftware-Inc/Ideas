# Structural Damage Hypothesis Falsifier

## Problem
A changed vibration or strain signature can be explained by damage, temperature, sensor drift, boundary-condition changes or load variation. Detection alone can create false alarms.

## Product
Generate competing structural explanations and actively seek measurements/tests that falsify them, producing ranked surviving damage hypotheses.

## Buyers
Infrastructure operators, structural-health-monitoring vendors and engineering consultancies.

## Material value
Reduce false maintenance interventions and missed damage.

## Moat
Mechanism-based hypothesis generation + experimental discrimination.

## MVP
Distinguish stiffness loss from temperature/sensor-bias scenarios in a simple structure.
