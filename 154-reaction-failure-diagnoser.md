# Reaction Failure Diagnoser

## Core idea
When a reaction underperforms, infer likely causes from the full experiment context rather than simply suggesting new conditions.

## Inputs
Reaction semantics, reagent lots, water/oxygen exposure, temperature profile, mixing, equipment, analytical traces, historical lab runs and literature evidence.

## Outputs
Ranked failure hypotheses such as mass-transfer limitation, catalyst deactivation, impurity poisoning, incorrect concentration, thermal excursion, workup loss or analytical misassignment, together with discriminating follow-up experiments.

## Buyers
Process development, synthetic chemistry, scale-up labs and autonomous laboratories.

## Economic value
Convert failed experiments into information. The product becomes more valuable as it accumulates local negative-result provenance that is usually lost in notebooks.
