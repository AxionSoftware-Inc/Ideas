# Downscaling Trust & Validity Mapper

## Core idea
For every local climate/environmental estimate produced by statistical, dynamical or AI downscaling, show where and why the result is trustworthy or extrapolative.

## Product
Compare source-model regime, local observations, terrain/coast/land-use context, calibration coverage and extreme-event behavior. Produce spatial/temporal validity maps and uncertainty warnings rather than a uniform confidence score.

## Buyers
Climate-risk services, infrastructure owners, insurers, agriculture/water planning, renewable-energy developers.

## Economic value
Local decisions depend on local extremes, but high spatial resolution can create false precision. A trust layer can be sold on top of existing climate data rather than competing with global climate-model centers.

## Synergy
Domain-of-Validity Mapper + Uncertainty Engine + geospatial provenance.
