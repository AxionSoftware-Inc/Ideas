# Research-to-Product Feasibility Compiler

## Core idea
Compile a scientific prototype into a structured commercialization readiness model: technical maturity, reproducibility, manufacturability, compute/hardware cost, integration burden, IP position, validation requirements and buyer-critical evidence.

## Product
Output a `GO / DE-RISK / LICENSE / SPINOUT / STOP` recommendation with explicit missing evidence and the cheapest experiments/prototypes that can change the decision.

## Buyers
TTOs, deep-tech founders, R&D labs, corporate venture teams and grant/innovation programs.

## Economic value
Research teams often spend money improving scientific performance that buyers do not care about while leaving integration, reliability or cost risks unresolved. The compiler directs scarce proof-of-concept funding to decision-relevant evidence.

## Synergy
R&D Portfolio Optimizer + Qualification Planner + IP Graph + Scientific Data Valuation.
