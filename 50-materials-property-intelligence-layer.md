# Materials Property Intelligence Layer

## Thesis
Create a federated property engine over materials databases, papers, simulations and internal company data.

Instead of returning one value such as `thermal conductivity = 148 W/mK`, return a property object containing:
- value distribution and uncertainty
- temperature/pressure/composition regime
- crystal phase and processing history
- measurement or simulation method
- source and provenance
- confidence and conflicts

## Product value
Engineers need properties under specific operating conditions, not generic handbook values.

## Business
Potential customers include materials R&D, batteries, aerospace, semiconductors, energy and manufacturing.

## Strategic value
This becomes a reusable property backend for material selection, simulation parameterization, inverse design and AI scientific agents.
