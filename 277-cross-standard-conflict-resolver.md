# Cross-Standard Conflict Resolver

## Idea
Detect conflicting, overlapping or differently defined requirements across multiple standards and jurisdictions.

## Capabilities
- definition alignment
- unit/threshold normalization
- normative conflict detection
- stricter-rule identification
- exception handling
- referenced-standard dependency analysis

## Buyers
Global manufacturers, compliance consultancies, certification bodies and regulators.

## Moat
Semantic comparison of machine-interpretable standards rather than keyword diffing.
