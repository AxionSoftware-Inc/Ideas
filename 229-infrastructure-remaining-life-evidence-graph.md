# Infrastructure Remaining-Life Evidence Graph

## Problem
Remaining-life estimates may combine inspection, materials, loads, deterioration models and expert assumptions, but the evidence chain is rarely transparent.

## Product
Represent each remaining-life conclusion as a dependency graph from raw observations through deterioration models, assumptions, uncertainty and interventions.

## Buyers
Infrastructure owners, insurers, engineering firms and regulators.

## Material value
More defensible maintenance and replacement decisions; easier audit and handoff across decades.

## Moat
Longitudinal evidence/provenance tied to asset state and model validity.

## MVP
Generate an auditable remaining-life graph for one corrosion/fatigue scenario.
