# Core Facility Queue & Pricing Planner

## Goal
Optimize shared scientific facility capacity and user fees without hiding congestion or cross-subsidies.

## Inputs
Instrument capacity, job durations, priority classes, staffing, maintenance, demand seasonality and recovery targets.

## Outputs
- expected waiting time
- capacity bottlenecks
- transparent cost-per-hour
- pricing scenarios
- reservation-policy effects
- when another instrument becomes justified

## MVP
Single shared microscopy facility.
