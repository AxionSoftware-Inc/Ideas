# Food Process Semantic IR

## Idea
Represent recipe, unit operation, material state, heat/mass transfer, microbial state, packaging, quality and safety constraints as one typed process object.

## Buyers
Food manufacturers, co-manufacturers and food-tech R&D.

## Value
Become the interoperability layer across process models, sensors, quality data and compliance evidence.

## MVP
Start with one processing chain and a small semantic schema. Do not replace solvers; own the semantic/evidence layer.
