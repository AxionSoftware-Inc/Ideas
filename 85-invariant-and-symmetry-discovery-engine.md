# Invariant & Symmetry Discovery Engine

## Thesis
Before trying to solve a model, automatically search for transformations and conserved quantities that reduce its effective complexity.

## Product
Given equations, operators or data, identify candidate:
- continuous/discrete symmetries
- invariants and first integrals
- conserved quantities
- equivariances
- gauge redundancies
- hidden coordinate transformations

## Pipeline
Generate transformations from typed operator/group families, test invariance symbolically/numerically, infer associated conserved structure when justified, and verify candidates.

## Value
- reduces equation search space
- improves symbolic regression
- reveals interpretable structure
- can simplify simulation and control
- useful in mechanics, PDEs, field theory and dynamical systems

## Moat
The discovered symmetry/invariant graph feeds governing-equation discovery, approximation, solver planning and theorem generation.
